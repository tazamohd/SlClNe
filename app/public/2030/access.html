03 — The honest arithmetic

What stops being paid for

The argument was never that it is cheap. It is that six things a workshop already pays for stop existing. This list is the same one the product makes today — none of it waits for 2030.

The second entry

The invoice was written once and the journal came from it. Nobody re-keys anything into a spreadsheet at day’s end, so nobody reconciles the difference at month’s end.

The paper estimate

Signed from the customer’s phone, in the language they read, before the car reaches the gate. Two days of waiting becomes half an hour.

The stock guess

The shelf orders against a forecast rather than a minimum, so the money sitting on it stops being a hedge against being wrong.

The compliance scramble

ZATCA correctness is a property of the invoice, not a project in the last week of the quarter.

The “who changed this”

Every change carries a row: who, when, from what, to what. The argument ends by reading rather than by seniority.

The follow-up that never happened

Reminders fire off the vehicle’s own condition, so the returning customer stops being a function of who remembered to call.

04 — Asked before

Questions

Are these real prices?
No. This page is a design study set in 2030 and every figure on it is invented. The product underneath — workshop management, e-invoicing, parts, CRM, HR and portals — is real and sold today. Ask for a quote against your own bay count and you will get a real number instead of a table.
Per branch, or per user?
Per branch, with a seat allowance inside it. Charging per user punishes a workshop for giving a technician an account, and a technician without an account is how you end up with a paper job card in a drawer — which is the exact problem this is meant to end.
Where does our data live?
On Workshop and Group, with us. On Enterprise, inside your own boundary under your own agreements. In all three, the export runs whenever you ask, in full, in a format somebody else can read — that is not a tier feature and never will be.
Do the roles change per tier?
The fourteen roles exist in all three. What changes is how many people may hold each at once, and whether a role may span more than one branch. Nothing about the permission model itself is bought or unlocked — that would make it a marketing surface instead of a boundary.
What happens if we stop paying?
The account goes read-only and the board stops proposing. Nothing is deleted, nothing is held hostage, and the export runs whether or not the invoice is paid. A system that takes your history away is not a system, it is a hostage arrangement.
Is Arabic a paid feature?
No, and it never has been. Arabic and English are both first-class in the product today, right to left included. A system sold in Saudi Arabia that priced Arabic as an add-on would deserve to be laughed out of the room.
