01 — The floor that thinks

Neural bay lattice

Nine bays, one mind. Each bay negotiates for the next vehicle against the others, bidding with tool availability, technician fatigue and the customer's stated hour of return. Nobody assigns work here; the floor settles it and writes the audit row afterwards.

02 — Diagnosis before arrival

Quantum diagnostic core

The core takes a vehicle's whole operating history — every torque event, every thermal minute, every road it ever complained about — and collapses it into a single signature. The technician does not read a fault code. They read a story with a confidence attached to it.

03 — Service comes to you

Autonomous service grid

No appointment, no drop-off. The grid dispatches a bay to the vehicle: a mobile cell rolls out from the nearest node, docks in the customer's parking bay overnight, and is gone before the commute. The workshop's four walls became a delivery radius.
