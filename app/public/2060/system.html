01 — The shape of it

Subsystem constellation

Nothing here is a layer stacked on another. Every subsystem talks to the core and to its neighbours directly, which is why an invoice can close a journal, a journal can flag a part and a part can move a bay's queue without a human carrying the message. Select a node.

03 — Who the lattice answers to

Fourteen standings

A role is not a menu; it is a boundary. In 2060 the lattice enforces it at the substrate, which is a longer way of saying what the product already does: every screen, every field and every write is checked against the standing of whoever asked, and the refusal is logged as carefully as the change.

04 — What holds it up

The spine

Four things carry everything above. Remove any one and the lattice is a filing cabinet.

S/01

The edge

Bays, tools, printers, mobile cells and the vehicles themselves. Everything that touches metal, reporting continuously rather than when someone remembers to.

S/02

The lattice

The negotiation layer. Work is bid for, not assigned. It is the bay board from 2025 with the authority to move itself.

S/03

The core

Reads a vehicle's whole life as one signature and hands the technician a story with a confidence attached, instead of a fault code with a page number.

S/04

The ledger

Every movement of money, matter or authority, written once, notarised at issue, and held by the customer for the life of the vehicle.
