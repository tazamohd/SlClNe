02 — How a part comes to exist

Matter, on demand

Four steps, none of which involve a lorry.

M/01

Licence

The core names the part and the supplier grants a single-use licence against it. This is the purchase order, and it still needs an approval before it spends anything.

M/02

Print

The geometry is cut in the bay. Forty minutes for a drive-unit housing, against eleven days when the same part had to be built somewhere else and carried here.

M/03

Certify

The printed part carries its own certificate — material batch, tolerances, the machine that made it and the technician who accepted it. A second signature, as always.

M/04

Book out

It leaves stock as it cools, and the ledger has it costed before it is bolted on. One write, one journal, no re-keying.
