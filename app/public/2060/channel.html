02 — After the handshake

What happens next

In the fiction. In reality, step one is where it ends, because this form goes nowhere.

C/01

The lattice answers

Within one cycle hour, on the return path you gave, from a human whose standing is printed at the top of the reply.

C/02

A bay is simulated

Your workshop is modelled on the grid — your bays, your job mix, your parts — and run forward for a synthetic month before anyone quotes you anything.

C/03

You stand in it

Twenty minutes inside the lattice with your own numbers in it, not a demo tenant with somebody else’s vehicles.

C/04

You decide, slowly

No cycle starts until you say so, and the first one can be ended with one cycle’s notice. Nothing here needs to be decided in the room.
