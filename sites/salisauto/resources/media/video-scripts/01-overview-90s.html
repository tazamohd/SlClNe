EN: SALIS AUTO runs the workshop from check-in to invoice on one record of the job, in Arabic and English, with ZATCA Phase 2 e-invoicing generated by the same transaction that posts the sale. Estimate approval at a three-branch workshop went from 48 hours on paper to 4 hours. Invoices at the counter from 15 minutes to 2. Book a 20-minute demo: salisauto.app
