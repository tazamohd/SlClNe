1. Purpose

This document defines the SALIS AUTO training program structure, delivery methods, scheduling, and success metrics. It serves as the master reference for all role-based training courses across the platform.

The training program ensures every user -- from Owner/CEO to external customers and suppliers -- can operate the SALIS AUTO platform confidently within their assigned scope and approval limits.

3. Training Tracks

The program is organized into four tracks, prioritized by go-live dependency.

TrackRolesDurationPriority
ExecutiveOwner/CEO, Branch Manager, Super Admin4-8h eachP0 (go-live)
OperationsService Advisor, Technician, QC Inspector4-8h eachP1 (week 1-2)
Back-OfficeAccountant, Storekeeper, Procurement, HR, Receptionist, Call Center8-10h combinedP2 (week 2-4)
ExternalCustomer, Supplier2-3h eachP3 (week 4-6)

3.1 Executive Track (P0)

Covers strategic oversight, financial controls, and platform administration. Must be completed before go-live.

  • Owner/CEO Course (SA-TRN-002): Dashboard & KPIs, Approvals (unlimited SAR), Reports & Strategic Analytics -- 4 hours
  • Branch Manager Course (SA-TRN-003): Branch Setup, Workshop Ops, Team Mgmt, Approvals (50,000 SAR), Reports -- 8 hours
  • Super Admin Course (SA-TRN-011): Platform Admin, RBAC Config, System Config, Security & Audit -- 6 hours

3.2 Operations Track (P1)

Covers day-to-day workshop operations and the full job lifecycle: Check-In, Inspection, Estimate, Repair, QC, Delivery.

  • Service Advisor Course (SA-TRN-004): Customer Reception, Inspection, Estimates (5,000 SAR), Job Management, Appointments -- 8 hours
  • Technician Course (SA-TRN-005): Job Assignment, Repair Workflow, Parts Requests, Documentation -- 6 hours
  • QC Inspector Course (SA-TRN-006): QC Checklist, Pass/Fail Workflow, Reporting -- 4 hours

3.3 Back-Office Track (P2)

Covers finance, inventory, procurement, HR, and support functions.

  • Finance Course (SA-TRN-007): Chart of Accounts, Invoicing & ZATCA, Payments, Parts Inventory, Procurement, Financial Reporting -- 10 hours
  • Support Staff Course (SA-TRN-008): Front Desk, Call Center, HR Management, CRM & Lead Management, Communication Tools -- 8 hours

3.4 External Track (P3)

Covers self-service portals for external stakeholders.

  • Customer Course (SA-TRN-009): Registration, Service Tracking, Invoices & Feedback -- 2 hours
  • Supplier Course (SA-TRN-010): Registration & Catalog, Order Management, Invoicing -- 3 hours

4. Delivery Methods

4.1 Instructor-Led Training (ILT)

  • Live sessions conducted by certified trainers
  • Maximum 12 participants per session
  • Bilingual delivery (EN/AR) with RTL interface demonstration
  • Hands-on labs using demo environment accounts
  • Recommended for Executive and Operations tracks

4.2 Self-Paced Learning

  • LMS-hosted video modules with interactive exercises
  • Progress tracking and auto-graded quizzes
  • Available 24/7 after initial enrollment
  • Recommended for External track and refresher training

4.3 Blended Learning

  • Combination of ILT for core modules and self-paced for supplementary content
  • Recommended for Back-Office track
  • Includes scheduled check-in sessions with trainers

7. LMS Overview

The SALIS AUTO LMS provides:

  • Course Catalog: All 14 courses organized by track
  • Progress Dashboard: Completion percentage, quiz scores, time spent
  • Certificate Generation: Automatic issuance upon passing assessments
  • Reporting: Trainer and admin dashboards for cohort-level analytics
  • Notifications: Email and in-app reminders for upcoming sessions and deadlines

Enrollment is managed by HR (hr@salisauto.sa) for internal users and via self-registration for external users.
