1.1 Learning Objectives

Upon completing this course, the Customer will be able to:

  1. Register and set up their customer portal account
  2. Book appointments, track services, and view vehicle history
  3. Review and pay invoices, and provide feedback on completed services

2. Module 1 -- Registration & Account Setup (40 minutes)

2.1 Topics

#TopicDuration
1Portal overview and access10 min
2Account registration10 min
3Profile setup and preferences10 min
4Vehicle registration10 min

2.2 Key Concepts

  • Self Scope: Customers can only view and manage their own records -- no access to other customers' data
  • Portal Access: Web and mobile-responsive portal with bilingual support (EN/AR, RTL)
  • Registration Methods: Self-registration via portal, or pre-registration by receptionist/advisor
  • Profile Fields: Name, phone, email, preferred language, preferred branch
  • Vehicle Linking: Customers can add multiple vehicles to their profile
  • Security: Password requirements, optional two-factor authentication

2.3 Hands-On Lab

Lab 1.1: Registering a New Account

  1. Navigate to the SALIS AUTO Customer Portal
  2. Click "Register" and fill in the required fields:
  3. Full name (EN and/or AR)
  4. Mobile phone number
  5. Email address
  6. Preferred language (English or Arabic)
  7. Set a password meeting the security requirements
  8. Verify the account via email or SMS verification code
  9. Log in with the new credentials

Lab 1.2: Setting Up Your Profile

  1. Log in with the demo account (khalid@example.sa / Demo@1234)
  2. Navigate to My Profile
  3. Review and update personal information
  4. Set the preferred branch for service
  5. Configure notification preferences (SMS, email, or both)
  6. Switch the portal language between English and Arabic

Lab 1.3: Adding a Vehicle

  1. Navigate to My Vehicles > Add Vehicle
  2. Enter the vehicle details:
  3. Plate number
  4. Make and model
  5. Year
  6. VIN (optional at registration)
  7. Current mileage
  8. Upload a photo of the vehicle (optional)
  9. Save the vehicle -- verify it appears in "My Vehicles"
  10. Add a second vehicle to demonstrate multi-vehicle support

2.4 Quiz -- Module 1

Q1. What scope does the customer role have?

  • A) Branch
  • B) All
  • C) Self (own records only)
  • D) External

Correct Answer: C -- Customers have "self" scope and can only access their own records.

Q2. Which languages does the customer portal support?

  • A) English only
  • B) Arabic only
  • C) English and Arabic (with RTL support)
  • D) English, Arabic, and French

Correct Answer: C -- The portal supports English and Arabic with full RTL layout.

Q3. How many vehicles can a customer register?

  • A) One only
  • B) Up to three
  • C) Up to five
  • D) Multiple (no fixed limit)

Correct Answer: D -- Customers can register multiple vehicles with no fixed limit.

3. Module 2 -- Service Tracking & Appointments (40 minutes)

3.1 Topics

#TopicDuration
1Booking an appointment10 min
2Viewing appointment history5 min
3Real-time service tracking10 min
4Service history and records10 min
5Notifications and alerts5 min

3.2 Key Concepts

  • Appointment Booking: Customers select branch, service type, vehicle, and preferred date/time
  • Availability Check: Real-time bay availability displayed during booking
  • Service Tracking: Live status updates on active jobs (Checked In, Under Inspection, In Repair, QC, Ready for Delivery)
  • Workshop Lifecycle Visibility: Customers see a simplified view of Check-In -> Inspection -> Estimate -> Repair -> QC -> Delivery
  • Service History: Complete history of all past services per vehicle
  • Notifications: Automated notifications at each status change via customer's preferred channel

3.3 Hands-On Lab

Lab 2.1: Booking an Appointment

  1. Log in to the customer portal (khalid@example.sa / Demo@1234)
  2. Navigate to Appointments > Book New
  3. Select a vehicle from "My Vehicles"
  4. Choose the service type (e.g., General Service, Repair, Inspection)
  5. Describe the concern or issue in the notes field
  6. Select the preferred branch
  7. View the available time slots on the calendar
  8. Confirm the appointment
  9. Verify the confirmation notification is received

Lab 2.2: Tracking an Active Service

  1. Navigate to My Services > Active
  2. Open an active job card (pre-loaded in the demo environment)
  3. View the current status on the visual lifecycle tracker
  4. Review any inspection findings or photos shared by the advisor
  5. Check the estimated completion time
  6. Navigate to the notification history to see past status updates

Lab 2.3: Reviewing Service History

  1. Navigate to My Vehicles > Select a vehicle > Service History
  2. Review the list of past services with dates, descriptions, and costs
  3. Click on a past service to view the detailed report
  4. Download a service record as PDF for personal records
  5. Note the mileage at each service for maintenance tracking

3.4 Quiz -- Module 2

Q1. What information is needed to book an appointment?

  • A) Vehicle, service type, and preferred date/time
  • B) Invoice number only
  • C) Technician preference
  • D) Payment method

Correct Answer: A -- Booking requires vehicle selection, service type, and preferred date/time.

Q2. Which lifecycle stages are visible to the customer?

  • A) Only Check-In and Delivery
  • B) Check-In, Inspection, Estimate, Repair, QC, Delivery
  • C) Only Repair and Delivery
  • D) Customers cannot track service status

Correct Answer: B -- Customers see a simplified view of all six lifecycle stages.

Q3. How are status updates delivered to customers?

  • A) Phone calls only
  • B) Automated notifications via SMS, email, or in-app based on preferences
  • C) Postal mail
  • D) Only visible in the portal

Correct Answer: B -- Automated notifications are sent via the customer's preferred channel.

4. Module 3 -- Invoices, Payments & Feedback (40 minutes)

4.1 Topics

#TopicDuration
1Viewing and understanding invoices10 min
2ZATCA QR code verification5 min
3Online payment options10 min
4Payment history and receipts5 min
5Submitting feedback and ratings10 min

4.2 Key Concepts

  • Invoice Access: Customers can view all invoices linked to their vehicles
  • ZATCA Compliance: Each invoice includes a QR code with VAT details (15% VAT)
  • Payment Methods: Online payment via the portal (card, bank transfer) or in-branch (cash, POS)
  • Currency Display: Amounts displayed in SAR (stored as integer halalas)
  • Receipts: Digital receipts available for download after payment
  • Feedback System: Star rating (1-5) and text comment per completed service
  • Feedback Impact: Customer feedback is linked to the job card, technician, and branch for quality tracking

4.3 Hands-On Lab

Lab 3.1: Reviewing an Invoice

  1. Navigate to My Invoices
  2. Open a recent invoice for a completed service
  3. Review the line items: labor, parts, VAT (15%)
  4. Verify the total amount in SAR
  5. Scan the QR code with a mobile device to verify ZATCA data
  6. Download the invoice as PDF

Lab 3.2: Making a Payment

  1. From the invoice view, click "Pay Now"
  2. Select the payment method (card or bank transfer)
  3. Enter payment details and confirm
  4. Verify the payment confirmation and receipt generation
  5. Navigate to Payment History to see the recorded transaction

Lab 3.3: Submitting Feedback

  1. Navigate to My Services > Completed
  2. Select a completed service that has no feedback yet
  3. Rate the service (1-5 stars)
  4. Add a text comment describing the experience
  5. Submit the feedback
  6. Verify the feedback appears in the service record

Lab 3.4: Viewing Estimate and Accepting

  1. Navigate to My Services > Active
  2. Open a job with status "Estimate Pending Approval"
  3. Review the estimate: line items, labor, parts, total
  4. Accept or reject the estimate from the portal
  5. If accepting, verify the job status changes to approved
  6. If rejecting, add a reason and discuss with the advisor

4.4 Quiz -- Module 3

Q1. What VAT rate is shown on SALIS AUTO invoices?

  • A) 5%
  • B) 10%
  • C) 15%
  • D) 20%

Correct Answer: C -- ZATCA Phase 2 mandates 15% VAT on all invoices.

Q2. What feedback options are available after a completed service?

  • A) Star rating only (1-5)
  • B) Text comment only
  • C) Star rating (1-5) and text comment
  • D) No feedback option exists

Correct Answer: C -- Customers can submit a star rating (1-5) and a text comment.

Q3. Can customers accept or reject estimates from the portal?

  • A) No, only in person
  • B) Yes, they can accept or reject with a reason
  • C) Only acceptance is available online
  • D) Estimates are not visible to customers

Correct Answer: B -- Customers can accept or reject estimates from the portal with a reason.

Q4. How are monetary amounts displayed in the portal?

  • A) In USD
  • B) In SAR with two decimal places
  • C) In halalas
  • D) In SAR rounded to nearest integer

Correct Answer: B -- Amounts are displayed in SAR with two decimal places (stored as integer halalas).

Q5. What payment methods are available through the portal?

  • A) Cash only
  • B) Card and bank transfer via the portal; cash and POS in-branch
  • C) Bank transfer only
  • D) Cryptocurrency

Correct Answer: B -- Online payments (card, bank transfer) via portal; cash and POS available in-branch.
