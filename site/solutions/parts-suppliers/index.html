How it works

From a workshop's parts request to your confirmed shipment

  1. Workshop raises a request

    A job card or stock reorder generates a purchase request against your catalog.

  2. You receive it in the portal

    The request appears in your incoming queue with quantities and reference numbers.

  3. You confirm or adjust

    Confirm full availability, part-fulfil, or flag items as backordered.

  4. Workshop reconciles on receipt

    Stock is received against your confirmed order, so quantities match on both sides.

  5. Invoice is matched

    Your invoice is reconciled against the same order, reducing disputes over what was actually delivered.

FAQ

Supplier questions

Do I need to be a Salisco customer to use the supplier portal?

You're invited by a workshop or distributor you already supply — the portal connects to their procurement workflow specifically.

Can I manage pricing per workshop?

Catalog and pricing management is part of the portal; specifics of per-customer pricing depend on your agreement with each workshop. Ask us during a demo.

Is this a public marketplace?

No — this connects you to workshops you already have a supply relationship with. It isn't an open marketplace for new customer discovery.
