One platform. Four connected audiences.

SalisAuto, Salisco's automotive product, is a single multi-tenant system that four different kinds of users work inside every day — a workshop's own team, the customers whose vehicles it services, the suppliers it buys parts from, and the owners or head offices managing more than one branch. Nobody re-enters the same job twice.

LiveWorkshops & service centersRun the floor: booking, diagnosis, job cards, QC, invoicing. In developmentVehicle ownersBook, approve and track service through a customer portal and app. In developmentParts & suppliersReceive purchase orders, manage a catalog, fulfil requests. In developmentEnterprise, fleets & insuranceMulti-branch control, RBAC, audit trail, claims workflows.

Why one platform

The workshop is the hub — everyone else connects to it

A job card created at check-in is the same job card the technician updates, the same one the customer approves from their phone, the same one that consumes parts from a supplier order, and the same one the invoice is generated from. Nothing is retyped between roles, which is the entire point of building this as one system instead of four separate tools.

Built for the Saudi aftermarket

Bilingual Arabic/English end to end, VAT-aware invoicing aligned to ZATCA e-invoicing rules, and halala-precision money handling — every amount is stored as an integer, never a rounded float.

Role-based, not one-size-fits-all

A permission matrix of 15 roles across 28 modules decides what each person can see and do — the same matrix the server enforces, not a front-end assumption. See how access control works →.

Honest about where it stands

The core workshop workflow — job cards, diagnosis, QC, parts, ZATCA invoicing — is the most mature part of the system. The customer, supplier and enterprise portals described below are built and screen-complete; connecting every one of them to live production data is ongoing engineering work, not finished polish.
