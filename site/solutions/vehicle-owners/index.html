In development — screens built, not yet live at every workshop

A documented service experience, not a verbal promise

The customer portal gives vehicle owners a record of what was agreed, what it costs, and where their vehicle is in the workshop — instead of a phone call and a memory of what was said.

Note for vehicle owners: this portal is offered by the workshop you take your vehicle to, not sold to you directly. If your workshop uses SalisAuto, they'll invite you to it. If you run a workshop and want to offer this to your customers, request a demo.

What changes for you

From "we'll call you" to a record you can check yourself

Book an appointment

Request a time slot at your workshop instead of calling and hoping someone picks up.

Approve estimates digitally

Review an itemized estimate and approve it with a tap — your approval and the time it happened are both recorded.

Track job progress

See where your vehicle is in the workflow instead of calling to ask.

Get a digital, VAT-compliant invoice

Receive an invoice tied to the exact job — not a hand-written total.

Keep a service history

Past jobs, parts and invoices stay attached to your vehicle record at that workshop.

Leave feedback

Rate the visit so the workshop — and, over time, you — can track service quality.

How it works

Five steps, all visible to you

  1. Book

    Request an appointment with the concern you want checked.

  2. Approve

    Review the diagnosis and estimate, and approve or query it before work starts.

  3. Track

    Follow the job through diagnosis, repair and quality control.

  4. Pay & receive your invoice

    Get a digital invoice that matches exactly what was approved.

  5. Rate the visit

    Leave feedback that becomes part of the workshop's record, not lost after a phone call.

FAQ

Vehicle owner questions

Do I need to create an account?

Registration is tied to a specific workshop's organization — your workshop sends you an invitation or registration link when they onboard you.

Is the portal available in Arabic?

Yes — the interface is bilingual Arabic/English with full right-to-left layout support, not a translated afterthought.

Is my data shared with other workshops?

No. Your records belong to the workshop's organization and are isolated from every other organization on the platform at the database level.

When will the mobile app be publicly available?

We'll publish store links here the moment they're live. We'd rather say "not yet" than send you to a page that doesn't exist.
