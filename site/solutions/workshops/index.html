How it works

One job card, from booking to invoice

  1. Booking & check-in

    An appointment or walk-in becomes a job card with the vehicle, customer and reported concern attached.

  2. Diagnosis & estimate

    The technician records findings; the system builds an itemized estimate from labour and parts.

  3. Customer approval

    The estimate is sent for approval and the response — and the timestamp — is recorded against the job card, not remembered.

  4. Job card execution

    Status moves through a guarded set of legal transitions — a job can't jump from "diagnosing" straight to "closed" by accident.

  5. Quality control

    A repair is signed off by someone other than the technician who performed it — the system enforces that separation, it isn't a house rule someone can skip under pressure.

  6. Parts & procurement

    Parts consumed on the job are drawn from stock or raised as a purchase order to a supplier, so the same part is never allocated twice.

  7. Invoicing & payment

    A VAT-aligned invoice is generated from the job card's actual labour and parts — not re-typed — and money is tracked to the halala.

FAQ

Workshop questions

Is my workshop's data isolated from other workshops on the platform?

Yes. Every query runs under a database row-level-security policy scoped to your organization, enforced with FORCE so even a superuser connection without the right context reads nothing. See Security & data.

Can a technician approve their own repair?

No. Quality control is a separate step assigned to someone other than the technician who performed the work — that's enforced by the system, not left to house policy.

Does it work on a tablet on the shop floor?

Most of the interface does. We're honest that tablet-specific layout verification is still in progress across the full screen set — ask us which screens are verified for your workflow.

Is SalisAuto live in production today?

The core workshop workflow described on this page is the most mature part of the system. Get in touch and we'll walk you through exactly what's ready for your use case.
