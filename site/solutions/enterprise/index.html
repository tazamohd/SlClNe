What changes for you

Consolidated visibility, branch-level accountability

Multi-branch organizations

Every branch operates its own jobs and stock; head office sees consolidated numbers without merging the underlying records.

Role-based access, enforced once

A single permission matrix of 15 roles across 28 modules is read by both the interface and the server — a test asserts the two copies never drift, so access rules can't quietly diverge.

Segregation of duties

The person who raises a financial document is never the one who approves it — enforced by the system on documents that move money.

An audit log that can't be edited

The audit trail rejects UPDATE and DELETE at the database grant level, for every role including administrators.

BI dashboards & financial statements

Consolidated reporting across branches, generated from the same records the workshop floor produces — not a separate spreadsheet exercise.

Nothing is hard-deleted

Financial and operational records are soft-deleted, not removed — so a mistake doesn't erase the trail that would explain it.

Insurance & loan workflows

Keep a claim or a financing case tied to the actual job

SalisAuto supports insurance and loan workflows attached to a job card, so the paperwork trail for a claim or a financed repair stays connected to the diagnosis, estimate and approval it's based on — rather than living in a separate folder. We haven't named specific insurers or lenders as integration partners here, because we're not going to claim a partnership we haven't verified in writing.

FAQ

Enterprise & fleet questions

Can I see all branches from one login?

Yes, for roles scoped to see across branches. Branch-scoped roles see only their own branch — the same permission matrix decides both.

Can a branch manager be restricted to their own branch?

Yes — that's one of the 15 roles in the permission matrix, and the restriction is enforced by the server, not just hidden in the interface.

Do you have named insurance or financing partners?

Not that we're publishing here. The platform supports the workflow; specific partner integrations would be named only once verified and agreed in writing.

Is referential integrity enforced at the database level?

Partially, and we'd rather say so than not: tenancy and audit-log append-only behaviour are enforced by the database itself, but a number of entity relationships are currently naming-convention links rather than foreign-key constraints. Ask us for specifics relevant to your use case.
