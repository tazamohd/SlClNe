Built around one workflow, shaped by who's running it

The underlying job-card-to-invoice workflow is the same everywhere. What changes by business type is which modules matter most, and who needs to see what.

Independent workshops

The problem: a paper job card, a spreadsheet for parts, WhatsApp for approvals.

What matters: booking & check-in, diagnosis & estimating, the technician portal, ZATCA-aligned invoicing.

See the workshop solution →

Multi-branch service centers

The problem: no consolidated view across locations; branch managers see only their own paper trail.

What matters: multi-branch organizations, role-scoped access, consolidated BI dashboards and financial statements.

See the enterprise solution →

Parts suppliers & distributors

The problem: orders relayed by phone, no visibility into fulfilment, mismatched deliveries.

What matters: the supplier portal's incoming order queue, catalog management, fulfilment status.

See the parts & suppliers solution →

Corporate fleets & leasing accounts

The problem: multiple vehicles across multiple workshops with no single service record.

What matters: multi-vehicle customer accounts, consolidated job history, financial documents that require sign-off.

See the enterprise solution →

Insurance & financing partners

The problem: a claim's paperwork lives separately from the diagnosis and estimate it's based on.

What matters: insurance and loan workflows tied directly to the job card. We don't name specific insurers here — see why on the enterprise page.

See insurance & loan workflows →
