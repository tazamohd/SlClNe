Home/Platform

One system, covering the whole operation

Booking and check-in, diagnosis and estimating, the job card, quality control, parts and procurement, ZATCA-aligned invoicing, payments and accounting, plus CRM, HR and payroll — with four portals facing outward: customer, technician, supplier and procurement.

Feature areas

What's covered, end to end

Operations

Booking, check-in, diagnosis, estimating, job cards with guarded status transitions, quality control.

Parts & procurement

Stock, purchase orders, the supplier portal's incoming order queue, reconciliation on receipt.

Financial

ZATCA-aligned VAT invoicing, payments, chart of accounts, journal entries, financial statements — segregation of duties on documents that move money.

CRM, HR & payroll

Customer records and feedback, staff records, payroll processing, tied to the same organization and branch model as everything else.

Portals

Customer, technician, supplier and procurement portals, plus a kiosk mode and a call-centre console for phone-based bookings.

Reporting

BI dashboards and financial statements generated from live operational data, not a separate export pipeline.
