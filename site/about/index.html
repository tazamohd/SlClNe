A technology company, engineering the Kingdom's next systems

Salisco is a Riyadh-based technology company building large-scale systems, mobile apps and applied AI, focused on two sectors: Automotive and Manpower (QHR). SalisAuto, our automotive product, is the first to ship.

Vision

To be Saudi Arabia's technology engine — building systems and AI that power entire industries, from the road to the workforce.

Mission

To design and ship dependable software, mobile apps and AI products that make our two sectors — Automotive and Manpower/QHR — faster, smarter and more reliable.

Structure

Three divisions: Technology (the software backbone), Manpower (talent and workforce solutions), and Automotive (SalisAuto, live today).

Where we are today

Told straight, not smoothed over

SalisAuto's core workshop workflow — booking, diagnosis, job cards, quality control, parts, ZATCA-aligned invoicing — is the most mature and tested part of the product. The customer, supplier and enterprise portals described throughout this site are built and their screens are complete; connecting all of them to live production traffic, at scale, in a real customer's environment is ongoing engineering work rather than finished polish.

We'd rather tell you that plainly during a demo than let a slide deck imply otherwise. See Security & Data for the specific parts of the architecture we're most confident about, and where we know there's more to do.
