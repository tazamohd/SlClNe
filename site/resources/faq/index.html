Frequently asked questions

Straight answers, including about the parts that aren't finished yet.

Is SalisAuto live in production?

The core workshop workflow — booking, job cards, quality control, parts, ZATCA-aligned invoicing — is the most mature part of the system. A number of customer-, supplier- and enterprise-facing screens are built and complete but are still being connected to live production data as we onboard customers. Ask us for the current status of the specific workflow you need.

Is my workshop's data isolated from other workshops on the platform?

Yes. Every table is governed by a database row-level-security policy scoped to your organization, enforced with FORCE so even a superuser connection without the right context reads nothing.

Is SalisAuto available in Arabic?

Yes — the interface is bilingual Arabic/English with full right-to-left layout support built in, not a translation applied on top.

Does SalisAuto handle ZATCA VAT compliance?

Invoicing is built to align with ZATCA e-invoicing rules, with totals computed server-side against a configured VAT rate — a client-supplied total is never trusted.

Is there a mobile app I can download?

Technician, customer and operational apps are packaged for native iOS and Android with Capacitor. We haven't published store links because we don't have a verified public listing yet — we'll link here the moment that changes.

Does it work on a tablet on the shop floor?

Most of the interface does. Tablet-specific layout verification across the full screen set is still in progress — ask us which screens are verified for your exact workflow.

How is pricing determined?

By your branches, team size and the modules you need — see Pricing. We don't publish a fixed rate card.

How do I get started?

Request a demo and tell us about your workshop or organization — we'll walk you through exactly what applies to you.
