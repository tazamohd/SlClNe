Privacy Policy

Applies to: www.saleisco.comLast updated: 16 September 2026

This page describes our actual practices on this marketing website in plain language. It is not legal advice, and we recommend it be reviewed by qualified counsel before being relied on for regulatory compliance. It covers www.saleisco.com only — the SalisAuto product application, once you sign up with an organization using it, is governed by that organization's own privacy terms, provided separately.

What we collect

This site collects information only when you choose to give it to us, through the Request a Demo or Contact forms: your name, company or workshop name, email and/or phone number, business type, approximate team or branch size, and any message you write.

We don't run analytics or advertising trackers on this site today. If that changes, this page will be updated first, and any tracking will respect the choices browsers and regulations require.

How submissions are handled

Depending on how this site is currently configured, a form submission either opens a pre-filled email in your own mail client addressed to hello@salisco.sa (nothing is sent until you send that email yourself), or is transmitted directly to our team's systems if a live intake service is configured. Either way, we don't sell or share what you submit with third parties for their own marketing.

Browser storage

We store one thing in your browser's local storage: your language preference (salisco-lang), so the site remembers whether you last viewed it in English or Arabic. It never leaves your browser and isn't linked to anything else.

Third-party resources

This site loads web fonts from Google Fonts. Loading a font from a third-party CDN means your browser makes a request to that provider's servers, which can see your IP address, in the same way any external resource on any website would.

Your choices

You can clear your browser's local storage at any time to remove your saved language preference. If you've contacted us and want your information removed from our records, email hello@salisco.sa and we'll act on that request.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@salisco.sa.
