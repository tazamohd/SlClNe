Before anything is posted

  • Palette law holds. No green, red, yellow, purple, pink or teal anywhere in the frame, including screenshots and stock imagery.
  • Orange means warning or live. It is never a decorative second colour. One orange element per design at most.
  • Numbers carry baselines. "48 h to 4 h", never "91% faster" on its own.
  • Arabic is written, not translated. A native speaker writes the Arabic variant from the brief, not from the English post.
  • Latin inside Arabic is isolated. Plates, ids, SKUs and SAR figures carry dir="ltr" in any rendered copy.
  • Currency is SAR, two decimals, mono. SAR 1,245.00, aligned when in a column.
  • No exclamation marks, no emoji. Emphasis comes from scale and colour, not punctuation.
  • Buttons are verb + object. "See what changed", "Book a demo". Never "Learn more", never "Click here".
  • Customer identity needs approval. Name, logo, plate or face only with written consent on file.
  • Logo clear space kept. One icon-height on every side; minimum 120 px wide; never rotated, recoloured or given effects.
  • Unannounced features stay unannounced. Only shipped functionality appears in a post.
  • Alt text on every image. One sentence stating what the image says, in the post's language.
