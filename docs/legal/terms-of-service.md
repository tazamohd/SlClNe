## 2. Service Description and Scope

2.1. SALIS AUTO provides a comprehensive automotive workshop management platform consisting of 28 modules organized across 13 domains, including Workshop Operations, Vehicle and Customer Registry, Finance and Invoicing, Accounting, CRM and Marketing, Administration, Authentication, AI Platform, Parts and Inventory, Call Center, Reports and Analytics, Team and HR, and Customer Portals.

2.2. The Service is provided as a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform with bilingual support in English and Arabic (including full RTL layout support).

2.3. The Service includes ZATCA Phase 2 e-invoicing integration, enabling Organizations to generate, submit, and manage electronic invoices in compliance with ZATCA regulations, including UBL 2.1 XML format, cryptographic hash chains, QR code generation, and real-time clearance.

2.4. The Service operates on a multi-tenant architecture with Row-Level Security (RLS) enforced across all 53 tenant-owned tables, ensuring complete data isolation between Organizations.

2.5. The specific modules and features available to an Organization depend on the Subscription plan selected pursuant to Section 4 of these Terms.
