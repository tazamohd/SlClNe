## 6. In-App Purchases and Payments

6.1. The App may facilitate payments for workshop services, including invoices for repairs, parts, and labor. All payments are processed through Stripe, our third-party payment processor.

6.2. All prices displayed in the App are in Saudi Riyals (SAR). Value Added Tax (VAT) at the rate of 15% is included or displayed separately as applicable, in accordance with ZATCA regulations.

6.3. All monetary calculations within the App are performed using integer halalas (1 SAR = 100 halalas) to ensure precision and prevent rounding errors.

6.4. Payment processing is subject to Stripe's terms of service and privacy policy. SALIS AUTO does not store credit card numbers or full payment card details.

6.5. Invoices processed through the App comply with ZATCA Phase 2 e-invoicing requirements, including QR code generation with TLV-encoded data (seller name, VAT number, timestamp, total, and VAT amount) and UBL 2.1 XML format.

6.6. Payment disputes shall be directed first to the subscribing workshop Organization and, if unresolved, to SALIS AUTO support at support@salisauto.com.
