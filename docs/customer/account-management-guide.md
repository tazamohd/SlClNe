## 1. Introduction

This guide covers everything you need to manage your SALIS AUTO account: organization settings, user management, subscription plans, billing, and account lifecycle. Whether you are setting up your account for the first time or managing an established multi-branch operation, this document has you covered.

---

## 2. Account Dashboard Overview

After logging in as an Owner or Super Admin, your dashboard provides a summary of your account:

| Section                | What You See                                              |
|------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| Organization Summary   | Your organization name, plan, and active branch count     |
| Usage Metrics          | Current users, storage used, SMS sent this month          |
| Billing Summary        | Current plan cost, next billing date, payment status      |
| Quick Actions          | Links to add users, manage branches, view invoices        |
| Recent Activity        | Latest system events across your organization             |

Navigate to **Settings** from the sidebar to access all account management features.

## 3. Organization Settings

### 3.1 Basic Information

Navigate to **Settings > General** to manage your organization profile:

| Field                  | Description                                              | Required |
|------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|----------|
| Organization Name (EN) | Your business name in English                            | Yes      |
| Organization Name (AR) | Your business name in Arabic                             | Yes      |
| Commercial Registration (CR) | Your CR number from the Ministry of Commerce      | Yes      |
| VAT Registration Number| Your 15-digit ZATCA VAT number                           | Yes      |
| Logo                   | Your business logo (displayed on invoices and reports)   | No       |
| Phone Number           | Primary contact number                                   | Yes      |
| Email                  | Primary business email                                   | Yes      |
| Address                | Business address (displayed on invoices)                 | Yes      |
| Timezone               | Arabia Standard Time (AST) by default                    | Yes      |

Your CR number and VAT number are used on all invoices and official documents generated by the platform. Make sure these are accurate to maintain ZATCA compliance.

### 3.2 ZATCA Settings

Navigate to **Settings > ZATCA** to configure your e-invoicing:

| Setting                | Description                                              |
|------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| Seller VAT Number      | Your organization's VAT registration number              |
| QR Code Generation     | Enabled by default for all invoices                      |
| Invoice Numbering      | Automatic sequential numbering per organization          |

ZATCA settings are configured during onboarding. Contact support if you need to make changes to your ZATCA configuration.

### 3.3 Branch Management

Navigate to **Settings > Branches** to manage your workshop locations.

#### Adding a Branch

1. Click **Add Branch**
2. Fill in the branch details:
   - Branch name (English and Arabic)
   - Address and phone number
   - Operating hours
   - Number of service bays
3. Assign a Branch Manager
4. Click **Save**

The new branch is immediately available. Staff assigned to this branch can begin using it right away.

#### Editing a Branch

Click on any branch card to update its details, view assigned staff, or check branch-level performance metrics.

#### Branch Limits by Plan

| Plan          | Branches Included | Additional Branches          |
|---------------|-------------------|------------------------------|
| Starter       | 1                 | Upgrade required             |
| Professional  | Up to 3           | SAR 800/month per additional |
| Enterprise    | Unlimited         | Included                     |

## 4. User Management

Navigate to **Admin > Users & Teams** to manage your team.

### 4.1 Inviting a New User

1. Click **Add User**
2. Fill in the user details:

| Field          | Description                                              |
|----------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| Full Name (EN) | User's name in English                                   |
| Full Name (AR) | User's name in Arabic                                    |
| Email          | Used for login -- must be unique                         |
| Phone          | Contact number                                           |
| Role           | Select from 14 available roles (see below)               |
| Branch         | Assign to a specific branch (for branch-scoped roles)    |
| Status         | Active or Inactive                                       |

3. Click **Save**
4. The user receives an email with their login credentials

### 4.2 Available Roles

| Role                | Typical Responsibilities                                 |
|---------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| Owner / CEO         | Full organization oversight, unlimited approvals         |
| Branch Manager      | Branch operations, approvals up to SAR 50,000            |
| Service Advisor     | Customer intake, estimates, job card creation             |
| Technician          | Repair work, task updates, knowledge base access         |
| QC Inspector        | Quality control checks, delivery approval                |
| Storekeeper         | Parts inventory, stock management                        |
| Accountant          | Invoicing, payments, financial reports                   |
| HR Manager          | Employee management, leave, payroll                      |
| Receptionist        | Front desk, walk-in customer registration                |
| Call Center Agent   | Phone inquiries, appointment booking                     |
| Procurement Agent   | Purchase orders, supplier management                     |

Each role sees only the screens and data relevant to their responsibilities. You cannot assign a role that exceeds your plan's available modules.

### 4.3 Editing a User

1. Find the user in the Users & Teams list (use the search bar to filter)
2. Click on their name to open their profile
3. Update any fields as needed
4. Click **Save**

### 4.4 Deactivating a User

When a team member leaves or no longer needs access:

1. Open the user's profile
2. Change **Status** to **Inactive**
3. Click **Save**

The user can no longer log in, but all their historical activity (job cards completed, invoices created, approvals given) remains in the system for audit purposes.

**Important:** Never delete a user who has performed actions in the system. Always deactivate instead to preserve your audit trail.

### 4.5 User Limits by Plan

| Plan          | Users Included | Additional Users                    |
|---------------|----------------|-------------------------------------|
| Starter       | Up to 10       | SAR 200/month per 10-user pack      |
| Professional  | Up to 50       | SAR 200/month per 10-user pack      |
| Enterprise    | Unlimited      | Included                            |

## 6. Billing and Invoices

### 6.1 Billing Cycle

| Billing Type | When You Are Billed                                      |
|--------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| Monthly      | On the 1st of each month                                |
| Annual       | On your subscription start date (one annual payment)     |

All prices are in Saudi Riyals (SAR). VAT at 15% is applied to all subscription fees per ZATCA regulations.

### 6.2 Payment Methods

SALIS AUTO accepts the following payment methods:

| Method          | Details                                              |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Mada            | Saudi debit card                                     |
| Credit Card     | Visa and Mastercard accepted                         |
| Bank Transfer   | Direct transfer to SALIS AUTO's bank account         |

To add or change your payment method:
1. Navigate to **Settings > Billing > Payment Methods**
2. Click **Add Payment Method** or edit an existing one
3. Follow the prompts to enter your payment details

### 6.3 Viewing Invoices

1. Navigate to **Settings > Billing > Invoice History**
2. View a list of all past invoices with dates, amounts, and payment status
3. Click any invoice to view details or download as PDF

Each subscription invoice includes:
- Subscription plan and period
- Any add-on modules
- Subtotal
- VAT (15%)
- Total amount
- Payment status

### 6.4 Payment Terms

| Term                   | Detail                                              |
|------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Payment due            | Net 15 days from invoice date                        |
| Late payment           | 2% monthly charge on overdue balances                |
| Failed payment         | 3 retry attempts, then account placed on hold        |

If your payment fails, you will receive email notifications. Your account remains accessible during the retry period but may be restricted if the balance remains unpaid.

## 7. Plan Upgrades and Downgrades

### 7.1 Upgrading Your Plan

You can upgrade your plan at any time:

1. Navigate to **Settings > Subscription**
2. Click **Change Plan**
3. Select the plan you want to upgrade to
4. Review the pricing difference
5. Confirm the upgrade

Upgrades take effect immediately. If you are on an annual plan, the price difference is prorated for the remaining months.

### 7.2 Downgrading Your Plan

Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle:

1. Navigate to **Settings > Subscription**
2. Click **Change Plan**
3. Select the plan you want to downgrade to
4. Review which features will no longer be available
5. Confirm the downgrade

**Before downgrading**, check that your current usage fits within the lower plan's limits:
- Number of active users
- Number of branches
- Storage used
- Modules in active use

Features not included in the lower plan will become read-only. Your historical data is preserved but you will not be able to create new records in those modules.

## 8. Adding and Removing Branches

### 8.1 Adding a Branch

If you need more branches than your plan includes:

- **Starter plan**: Upgrade to Professional (supports up to 3 branches)
- **Professional plan**: Add additional branches at SAR 800/month each
- **Enterprise plan**: Unlimited branches included

To add a branch beyond your plan's included count:
1. Navigate to **Settings > Subscription > Add-Ons**
2. Select **Additional Branch**
3. Complete the branch details
4. The add-on is billed starting from the next billing cycle

### 8.2 Removing a Branch

1. Navigate to **Settings > Branches**
2. Select the branch you want to remove
3. Reassign any active job cards, staff, and inventory to another branch
4. Click **Deactivate Branch**

The branch's historical data (completed job cards, invoices, etc.) remains accessible in reports. The add-on charge for the removed branch stops at the next billing cycle.

## 9. Account Cancellation

### 9.1 How to Cancel

1. Navigate to **Settings > Subscription**
2. Click **Cancel Subscription**
3. Select a reason for cancellation (helps us improve)
4. Review the cancellation terms
5. Confirm cancellation

### 9.2 Cancellation Terms

| Billing Type | Notice Required | When Cancellation Takes Effect          |
|--------------|-----------------|------------------------------------------|
| Monthly      | 30 days         | End of the current billing month         |
| Annual       | 30 days         | End of the current annual term           |

Annual subscriptions are not refunded mid-term. Your access continues until the end of the paid period.

### 9.3 What Happens After Cancellation

| Period                    | What Happens                                          |
|---------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| Until end of paid period  | Full access continues                                 |
| 30 days after closure     | Data export available on request                      |
| 90 days after closure     | Non-financial data permanently deleted                |
| 7 years after closure     | Financial records and ZATCA invoices deleted           |

**Before canceling**, we recommend:
1. Exporting all data you may need (see [Data Portability Guide](data-portability-guide.md))
2. Downloading copies of all invoices as PDF
3. Saving any custom reports

### 9.4 Reactivating a Canceled Account

If you change your mind within 90 days of cancellation, contact support@salisauto.sa to reactivate your account. Your data will be restored to the state it was in at the time of cancellation.

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I start with Starter and upgrade later?**
A: Yes. Upgrades are immediate and take effect right away. If you are on an annual plan, the price difference is prorated.

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
A: We offer a 14-day guided trial with demo data so you can explore the platform before committing. Contact sales@salisauto.sa to get started.

**Q: What happens if I exceed my storage limit?**
A: You will receive a notification when you reach 80% and 90% of your storage limit. You can purchase additional storage at SAR 100/month per 10 GB, or upgrade your plan.

**Q: Can I transfer my account to a new owner?**
A: Yes. Contact support@salisauto.sa to initiate an ownership transfer. Both the current and new owner must verify their identity.

**Q: How do I change my organization's VAT number?**
A: Navigate to Settings > ZATCA and update your VAT registration number. The change takes effect on the next invoice generated. Contact support if you need assistance.

**Q: What payment currencies do you accept?**
A: All billing is in Saudi Riyals (SAR) only. Multi-currency billing is not currently available.
