Integration Patterns

How data flows between bounded contexts at SALIS AUTO

Core Flow

Workshop-Centric Hub

Workshop is the core domain receiving customer data from Registry, parts from Inventory, and technician assignments from HR. Job completion triggers billing in Finance.

Financial Pipeline

Finance to Accounting

Invoices and payments in Finance generate journal entries pushed through an anti-corruption layer into the Accounting domain's chart of accounts and ledgers.

Supply Chain

Procurement to Inventory

Purchase orders flow from Procurement to suppliers. Goods receipts update Inventory stock levels, which feed parts availability into the Workshop domain.

Portal Boundaries

Anti-Corruption Layers

Customer and Supplier portals communicate through ACLs that translate external-facing models into internal domain models, preventing external concerns from leaking inward.
