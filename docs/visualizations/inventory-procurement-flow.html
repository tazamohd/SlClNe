Reorder Automation Feature Flag

Toggle auto-PO generation for items that fall below reorder point

ff_auto_po
When enabled, the system automatically generates purchase orders for parts that fall below their configured reorder point during the daily stock scan. POs are routed through the standard approval chain.
DISABLED

PO Approval Escalation Chain

Role-based authorization limits for purchase order approval (SAR)

< SAR 10,000
Parts Manager
Standard parts orders within this limit. Routine reorder approvals for stock replenishment.
< SAR 20,000
Procurement
Escalated orders exceeding parts manager limit. Bulk purchases and non-standard vendor orders.
< SAR 50,000
Branch Manager
High-value procurement requiring management review. Capital equipment and specialty parts.
Unlimited
Owner / Admin
No upper limit. Full authorization on any procurement amount. Complete audit trail maintained.

Stock Status Indicators

Visual indicators based on current stock level relative to reorder point

Healthy Stock
Above Reorder Point
Current quantity exceeds reorder point by comfortable margin. No action required. Standard monitoring continues.
Warning Level
Approaching Reorder
Stock is between reorder point and safety stock. Auto-PO triggered if ff_auto_po enabled. Manual review recommended.
Critical Stock
Below Safety Stock
Urgent replenishment needed. Jobs may be delayed. Emergency PO escalated to management. SMS alert to parts manager.
