Customer Portal Access Only

Customers have no RBAC module access. They interact exclusively through the customer portal, seeing only their own vehicles, bookings, estimates, invoices, and service history. No workshop internal data is exposed.

Customer Touchpoints

Booking Confirmation

SMS and push notification with booking reference, date, time, and branch address. Calendar invite attachment.

Vehicle Check-In

Notification when vehicle is checked in. Mileage and initial photos visible in app. Service advisor name assigned.

Estimate Ready

Detailed estimate with line items, parts, labor, 15% VAT breakdown. Total in SAR. E-signature required to proceed.

Repair In Progress

Live tracking begins. Stage transitions trigger push notifications. Estimated time remaining displayed in app.

Ready for Pickup

QC passed notification. Invoice available for online payment. Branch location and hours displayed for pickup scheduling.

Feedback Request

24 hours after delivery: NPS survey, star rating, and optional written review. Results feed into workshop analytics.

Real-Time Tracking

Push notifications at every stage transition. Visual progress bar shows current stage with estimated time remaining. Technician name visible during active repair.

Stripe Payment

Integrated payment via Stripe supporting credit cards (Visa/MC), mada (Saudi debit network), and Apple Pay. Automatic receipt generation with ZATCA-compliant QR code.

E-Signature

Estimate approval via digital signature. Customers review line-item details and VAT breakdown before signing. Rejection option with free-text comments for negotiation.

NPS & Reviews

Post-service Net Promoter Score survey and written review. Feedback feeds into workshop performance analytics and technician quality metrics.
