Supplier Portal Journey

External supplier workflow for purchase orders, delivery management, invoicing, and payment tracking through a scoped portal view.

Scoped Portal Access

Suppliers see only Purchase Orders and payments related to their own account. They have no visibility into workshop operations, other suppliers, customer data, or internal financial records. The portal is a walled-off view designed for external collaboration.

Three-Way Match Process

1. Purchase Order

Original PO with agreed parts, quantities, and unit prices in SAR.

2. Delivery Note

Packing list confirming items shipped. Verified at workshop goods receipt.

3. Supplier Invoice

Invoice amounts validated against PO prices and received quantities.

All three documents must match in quantities and pricing before payment is approved. Discrepancies are flagged for manual review and resolution between supplier and workshop procurement.

PO Management

View, confirm, or reject purchase orders. Partial acceptance supported for backorder scenarios. PO details include parts, quantities, unit prices, and delivery deadlines.

Delivery Tracking

Update shipment status through the portal. Workshop receives real-time visibility on expected arrival. Packing list attachment supports three-way matching at goods receipt.

Invoice Submission

Submit invoices digitally through the portal. System validates against confirmed PO and goods receipt (three-way match). SAR amounts and 15% VAT auto-verified.

Payment Transparency

Track payment lifecycle from submission through approval to settlement. View payment terms, expected dates, and historical statements. Export data for supplier-side accounting.
