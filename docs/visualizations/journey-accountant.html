Accountant User Journey

Financial workflow spanning invoicing, ZATCA e-invoicing compliance, payment recording, journal management, and month-end close.

ZATCA Phase 2 E-Invoicing Integration

Every invoice generates a ZATCA-compliant XML document with QR code and cryptographic hash chain. Invoices are submitted to ZATCA for clearance before delivery to customers. The system tracks submission status and handles rejections with resubmission workflow.

SOD: Journal Posting vs. Approval

The accountant who posts a journal entry cannot approve it. A different authorized user (Finance Manager or Branch Owner) must review and approve. Approval ceiling for accountants is SAR 25,000 -- amounts above require Finance Manager approval.

ZATCA E-Invoice Workflow

1

Generate XML

Invoice data converted to UBL 2.1 XML format with ZATCA-mandated fields.

2

Sign & Hash

Cryptographic signing with CSID. Hash chain links to previous invoice for tamper-proofing.

3

Generate QR

TLV-encoded QR with seller name, VAT number, timestamp, totals, and VAT amount.

4

Submit

Submit to ZATCA for clearance. Track status: pending, cleared, or rejected with error details.

Approval Ceiling Context

Role Ceiling (SAR) Can Approve
Technician 0 Nothing financial
Service Advisor 5,000 Small parts, consumables
Accountant 25,000 Invoices, payments, journals (not own)
Finance Manager 100,000 Large POs, journal approvals
Branch Owner Unlimited All financial transactions

VAT Calculation

15% VAT applied automatically to all taxable line items. Separate tracking of exempt items. VAT return preparation with ZATCA-compliant reporting periods.

ZATCA QR & Hash Chain

Each cleared invoice carries a unique QR code with seller info, VAT number, timestamp, and amounts. Cryptographic hash chain ensures tamper-proof invoice sequencing.

AR Aging Reports

Track outstanding receivables by 30/60/90/120+ day buckets. Identify overdue accounts. Support collection workflows with automated reminders.

Month-End Close

Structured close process: verify journals, reconcile banks, review accruals, lock period. Prevents backdated entries after close. Generates close checklist for audit trail.
