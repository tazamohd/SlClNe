QC Inspector User Journey

Quality control workflow ensuring every repair meets standards before customer delivery. The last gate in the workshop lifecycle.

Quality Gate -- Last Line of Defense

QC is the final checkpoint before customer delivery. No vehicle leaves the workshop without QC sign-off. The multi-point checklist covers safety, functionality, cosmetics, and test drive results. Failed items generate rework orders automatically.

SOD: Technician cannot equal QC Inspector

The system enforces that the person performing QC on a job must be different from the technician who performed the repair. This prevents self-certification and ensures independent quality validation.

Inspection Checklist Categories

Safety

Brakes, seatbelts, airbag indicators, tire condition, lights, horn, steering response.

Mechanical

Engine performance, transmission, suspension, exhaust, cooling system, belt tensions.

Electrical

Battery, alternator, wiring, dashboard indicators, A/C, infotainment, sensors, OBD codes.

Cosmetic

Paint finish, panel alignment, trim condition, glass integrity, interior cleanliness post-repair.

Fluids

Oil level, coolant, brake fluid, power steering, windshield washer, transmission fluid levels and condition.

Road Test

Test drive verification: noise, vibration, shifting, braking distance, alignment tracking, acceleration response.

Pass Outcome

  • Inspector digital signature recorded
  • Job status set to "QC Passed"
  • Job moves to Delivery stage
  • Customer notified: vehicle ready for pickup
  • Service advisor prepares final documentation

Fail Outcome

  • Failed items documented with notes
  • Job returns to Repair queue
  • Technician receives rework notification
  • Rework counter incremented (analytics)
  • After rework, job returns to QC queue

Multi-Point Checklist

Configurable checklist per service type. Each item has pass/fail toggle with mandatory notes on failure. Categories cover safety, mechanical, electrical, cosmetic, and road-test criteria.

Photo Documentation

QC inspector can capture photos of completed work, comparing against technician's before-photos. Visual evidence supports customer transparency and dispute resolution.

Rework Tracking

Failed QC items generate rework orders with specific failure details. The system tracks rework count per technician for performance analytics and training needs assessment.

Digital Sign-Off

QC sign-off is a formal digital signature creating an audit trail. The inspector's identity, timestamp, and checklist results are permanently recorded for regulatory compliance.
