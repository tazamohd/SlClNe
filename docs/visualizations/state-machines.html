Guard Conditions

Business rules enforced at state transitions

SAR Ceiling (Job Card)

Estimate approval requires role-based SAR limits: Advisor up to 5,000 SAR, Manager up to 50,000 SAR, Owner unlimited.

SAR Ceiling (Purchase Order)

PO approval escalates by value: Parts Manager up to 10K SAR, Procurement up to 20K, Manager up to 50K SAR.

Separation of Duties

QC inspector cannot be the technician who performed the repair. PO approver cannot be the same person who created the requisition.

Customer Signature

Vehicle delivery requires a captured customer signature confirming the handover and acceptance of completed work.
