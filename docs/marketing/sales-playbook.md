## 1. Introduction

This playbook equips the SALIS AUTO sales team with the frameworks, messaging, and tools needed to identify, qualify, and close automotive workshop management deals across Saudi Arabia. It covers ideal customer profiles, buyer personas, sales methodology, objection handling, and follow-up processes.

---

## 2. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### 2.1 Primary ICP Characteristics

| Attribute              | Ideal Profile                                          |
|------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| Industry               | Automotive workshop, maintenance center, fleet service |
| Location               | Saudi Arabia (primary: Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam)        |
| Size                   | 1-20 branches, 5-200+ employees                       |
| Current Systems         | Paper-based, Excel, or legacy desktop software        |
| ZATCA Status           | Non-compliant or partially compliant with Phase 2     |
| Revenue                | SAR 500,000 - SAR 50,000,000+ annual                  |
| Decision Timeline      | 1-6 months                                            |
| Technology Readiness   | Basic internet infrastructure, smartphone users       |

### 2.2 Disqualification Criteria

| Signal                                               | Action                           |
|------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| No internet connectivity at workshop                 | Defer until infrastructure ready |
| Already contracted with CDK/SAP (>2 years remaining) | Long-term nurture track          |
| Single-person operation with no employees            | Not a fit -- too small           |
| Looking for free/open-source only                    | Nurture with ZATCA penalty angle |
| Non-automotive business                              | Redirect to appropriate solution |

### 2.3 Segmentation Tiers

| Tier         | Branches | Employees | Ticket Size (ARR) | Sales Motion     |
|--------------|----------|-----------|--------------------|------------------|
| SMB          | 1        | 5-15      | SAR 12,000-18,000  | Self-serve + call|
| Mid-Market   | 2-5      | 15-60     | SAR 30,000-60,000  | Inside sales     |
| Enterprise   | 6+       | 60+       | SAR 120,000+       | Field sales + SE |

## 4. Sales Process Stages

### 4.1 Stage Definitions

| Stage         | Activities                                    | Exit Criteria                       | Avg Duration |
|---------------|-----------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|--------------|
| Prospecting   | Identify target, initial outreach              | Meeting booked                      | 1-2 weeks    |
| Discovery     | Understand pain points, map stakeholders       | BANT qualified                      | 1-2 weeks    |
| Demo          | Platform demonstration (SA-MKT-006)            | Stakeholder buy-in                  | 1 week       |
| Proposal      | Pricing, ROI analysis, proposal delivery       | Proposal reviewed                   | 1-2 weeks    |
| Negotiation   | Terms discussion, contract review              | Terms agreed                        | 1-2 weeks    |
| Closed Won    | Contract signed, implementation handoff        | Signed agreement                    | --           |
| Closed Lost   | Document reasons, enter nurture track          | Loss reason recorded                | --           |

### 4.2 Stage Conversion Benchmarks

| Transition                | Target Rate | Actions if Below Target                    |
|---------------------------|-------------|---------------------------------------------|
| Prospect → Discovery      | 25%         | Refine targeting, improve outreach messaging|
| Discovery → Demo          | 60%         | Improve qualification, sharpen pain mapping |
| Demo → Proposal           | 70%         | Enhance demo, tailor to prospect needs      |
| Proposal → Negotiation    | 50%         | Review pricing, strengthen ROI narrative    |
| Negotiation → Closed Won  | 60%         | Address objections, provide references      |

## 5. BANT Qualification Framework

### 5.1 Qualification Criteria

| Dimension  | Question to Ask                                          | Qualified Answer                    |
|------------|----------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Budget     | "What budget have you allocated for workshop management systems?" | SAR 12,000+ annual or willingness to invest |
| Authority  | "Who makes the final decision on technology purchases?"  | Access to decision maker identified |
| Need       | "What is your biggest operational challenge today?"      | Matches SALIS AUTO value propositions|
| Timeline   | "When do you need to have a solution in place?"          | Within 6 months                     |

### 5.2 Scoring Matrix

| Score  | Budget         | Authority       | Need            | Timeline        |
|--------|----------------|-----------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| 3      | Defined, fits   | Direct contact  | Critical pain   | Within 3 months |
| 2      | Flexible        | Through referral| Moderate pain   | 3-6 months      |
| 1      | Unknown         | Unknown         | Nice-to-have    | 6+ months       |
| 0      | No budget       | No access       | No fit          | No urgency      |

**Qualification threshold**: Total score of 8+ out of 12 to proceed to Demo stage.

## 7. Objection Handling

### 7.1 Top 10 Objections and Responses

| # | Objection | Response |
|---|-----------|----------|
| 1 | "We cannot afford it right now." | "I understand budget is a concern. Let me put it in perspective: SALIS AUTO Starter is SAR 999/month. A single ZATCA non-compliance penalty starts at SAR 5,000. Beyond compliance, the average workshop sees a 25% throughput increase -- that is SAR 600,000+ in additional annual revenue for a small workshop. The platform pays for itself in the first month. Can I walk you through the ROI model with your specific numbers?" |
| 2 | "We are doing fine with paper and Excel." | "Many of our customers said the same thing before ZATCA Phase 2. Paper and Excel cannot generate compliant e-invoices with QR codes and hash chains. Beyond compliance, consider the hidden costs: data re-entry errors, lost job cards, no real-time visibility, and hours spent on manual reporting. What would it mean for your business if you could see every branch's performance in real time?" |
| 3 | "We already use another system." | "Which system are you using? Many workshops find that general-purpose tools were not built for Saudi automotive operations. They lack native ZATCA Phase 2 compliance, Arabic RTL support, and SAR halala precision. How much customization and manual workaround does your team do today? SALIS AUTO was purpose-built for exactly your business." |
| 4 | "Implementation will disrupt our operations." | "We designed our implementation process to avoid disruption. Most workshops go live in 4-8 weeks with a phased approach. We run parallel operations during transition -- your team continues working as usual while we set up the new system. Training is role-based and takes 1-2 days per role. Would you like to hear how Al-Rashid Auto Services went live across 5 branches without losing a single day of operations?" |
| 5 | "My team will not adopt new technology." | "That is a valid concern, and we have seen it before. SALIS AUTO was designed for workshop staff, not IT professionals. The interface is available in Arabic, and we provide hands-on training for each role. Our demo accounts let your team explore the system before committing. In most workshops, technicians and advisors are comfortable within the first week. Can we set up a trial for your team?" |
| 6 | "We need to think about it." | "Of course. To help you evaluate, I would like to send you three things: demo credentials so your team can explore the platform, an ROI analysis with your specific numbers, and a customer reference in your segment. What is the best way to follow up -- and when would be a good time for a second conversation?" |
| 7 | "The pricing is too high." | "Let me understand what you are comparing against. Our Starter plan at SAR 999/month covers your entire branch -- that is less than the cost of one admin employee for one day. The Professional plan at SAR 2,499/month covers up to 3 branches with full ZATCA compliance and AI capabilities. We also offer a 15% discount for annual commitment. What budget range were you considering?" |
| 8 | "We want to wait for a local/cheaper solution." | "ZATCA Phase 2 compliance is not optional, and penalties accumulate per invoice. Every month without a compliant system increases your regulatory exposure. SALIS AUTO is the local solution -- built in Saudi Arabia, for Saudi workshops, with Saudi regulatory compliance built in. What specific requirements would you want to see in a solution before moving forward?" |
| 9 | "Can you guarantee results?" | "We share our platform metrics transparently: 25% throughput increase, 91% faster estimate approvals, 87% faster invoicing. These are measured across real deployments. While every workshop is different, we offer a structured implementation with clear milestones and success metrics. We will work with you to define what success looks like for your specific operation." |
| 10 | "We need to involve other stakeholders." | "Absolutely -- important decisions should involve the right people. Who else should be part of this evaluation? We can arrange a tailored demo for your finance team focusing on ZATCA compliance, or a technical walkthrough for your operations team. We also have demo credentials for 14 different roles so each stakeholder can explore their specific view." |

## 8. Demo Booking Process

### 8.1 Booking Flow

| Step | Action                                                        | Owner    |
|------|---------------------------------------------------------------|----------|
| 1    | Qualify prospect using BANT framework (Section 5)             | Sales    |
| 2    | Identify key stakeholders who should attend                   | Sales    |
| 3    | Schedule 45-minute demo slot                                  | Sales    |
| 4    | Send calendar invite with pre-demo questionnaire              | Sales    |
| 5    | Review prospect's questionnaire responses                     | Sales    |
| 6    | Customize demo talking points per prospect pain points        | Sales/SE |
| 7    | Conduct demo following SA-MKT-006 (Demo Script)               | Sales/SE |
| 8    | Send follow-up within 24 hours                                | Sales    |

### 8.2 Pre-Demo Questionnaire

| # | Question                                                      |
|---|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1 | How many branches and bays does your operation have?          |
| 2 | What is your primary challenge: compliance, efficiency, or growth? |
| 3 | What systems do you currently use for workshop management?    |
| 4 | Who will be attending the demo and what are their roles?      |
| 5 | Is there a specific timeline driving your evaluation?         |
