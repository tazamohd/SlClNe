## 5. Boilerplate Texts

### 5.1 30-Word Boilerplate

SALIS AUTO is a multi-tenant automotive workshop management SaaS platform purpose-built for Saudi Arabia, delivering ZATCA-compliant invoicing, AI-powered operations, and bilingual Arabic/English support across 191+ screens.

### 5.2 50-Word Boilerplate

SALIS AUTO is a comprehensive automotive workshop management SaaS platform designed for the Saudi Arabian market. Spanning 191+ screens across 13 integrated domains, the platform delivers native ZATCA Phase 2 e-invoicing compliance, AI-powered scheduling and analytics, full Arabic RTL support, and a mobile customer portal -- transforming workshop operations from paper-based processes to digital excellence.

### 5.3 100-Word Boilerplate

SALIS AUTO is a multi-tenant automotive workshop management SaaS platform purpose-built for Saudi Arabia. The platform covers the complete workshop lifecycle -- from vehicle check-in through inspection, estimate, repair, quality control, and delivery -- across 191+ screens organized into 13 integrated domains including Workshop, Finance, CRM, AI Platform, and Parts & Inventory.

Key capabilities include native ZATCA Phase 2 e-invoicing with QR codes and hash chain integrity, AI-powered scheduling and analytics, full bilingual Arabic/English operation with RTL support, and a mobile-optimized customer portal with SMS-verified e-signatures. SALIS AUTO supports 14 organizational roles through a granular 28-module RBAC framework, serving independent workshops through enterprise fleet operations.

## 9. Visual and Social Media Guidelines

### 9.1 Photography

All imagery should be clean, professional, and well-lit, showing real workshop environments and the Saudi workforce. Use platform screenshots cropped from the demo environment (never showing identifiable customer data). Minimum resolution: 300 DPI (print), 72 DPI (web). Always obtain consent for photographs featuring identifiable individuals. Do not use stock photos that feel generic, images from competitor platforms, or images that do not reflect the Saudi market context.

### 9.2 Social Media

| Platform        | Handle              | Content Focus                        |

| LinkedIn        | SALIS AUTO           | Industry insights, product updates   |
| Twitter/X       | @SalisAuto           | News, tips, engagement               |
| Instagram       | @salisauto           | Visual content, customer stories     |
| YouTube         | SALIS AUTO           | Demos, tutorials, webinars           |

Maintain professional tone. Post in both English and Arabic. Use hashtags: #SalisAuto #WorkshopManagement #ZATCA #Vision2030. Do not share unannounced features or engage in competitive commentary. Get approval before posting customer names or logos.

## 11. Media Inquiry Handling

### 11.1 Response Protocol

| Step | Action                                              | Timeline        |
|------|-----------------------------------------------------|-----------------|
| 1    | Log inquiry in PR tracker                           | Immediately     |
| 2    | Acknowledge receipt to journalist                   | Within 2 hours  |
| 3    | Route to appropriate spokesperson                   | Within 2 hours  |
| 4    | Prepare talking points and approve response         | Within 4 hours  |
| 5    | Deliver response to journalist                      | Within 24 hours |
| 6    | Log outcome and archive correspondence              | Post-interaction|

### 11.2 Spokesperson Authorization

Company strategy and financials: CEO or CFO only. Product announcements: CEO or VP Product. Customer relationships: VP Sales (with customer consent). Technical architecture: CTO or VP Engineering. Partnerships: VP Business Development.

Off-limits topics for all spokespeople: unannounced product features or roadmap, revenue/valuation details, specific customer names without consent, competitor criticisms, speculation on regulations or government policy, and internal personnel matters.
