## 1. Introduction

This document provides an analysis of the Saudi Arabian automotive aftermarket with focus on the workshop management software opportunity. It covers market sizing, regulatory drivers, regional dynamics, competitive forces, and the Total Addressable Market (TAM), Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM), and Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) for SALIS AUTO.

## 3. Regulatory Environment

### 3.1 ZATCA Phase 2 E-Invoicing

| Aspect                    | Detail                                                   |
|---------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| Mandate                   | Electronic invoicing required for all VAT-registered entities |
| Phase 1 (Generation)      | Effective December 4, 2021 -- invoice generation         |
| Phase 2 (Integration)     | Phased rollout by revenue threshold, 2023-2025           |
| Coverage by 2026          | All VAT-registered businesses with revenue > SAR 0       |
| Requirements              | UBL 2.1 XML, TLV QR codes, hash chain, Fatoora API      |
| Penalties                 | SAR 5,000+ per non-compliant invoice                     |
| Impact on Workshops       | Most workshops now required to issue compliant e-invoices|

### 3.2 ZATCA Compliance Landscape (Workshops)

| Status                     | Estimated Share | Opportunity                            |
|----------------------------|-----------------|----------------------------------------|
| Fully Compliant            | 15-20%          | Upgrade and efficiency opportunity     |
| Partially Compliant        | 25-30%          | Integration and automation opportunity |
| Non-Compliant              | 50-55%          | Urgent compliance + digitization need  |

### 3.3 Other Regulatory Factors

| Regulation                         | Impact on Workshops                                  |
|------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| VAT at 15%                         | Requires accurate tax calculation on every invoice   |
| Saudization (Nitaqat)              | Staffing mix requirements, HR management needs       |
| Vehicle Inspection Standards       | Quality standards for maintenance operations         |
| Environmental Regulations          | Waste management and emissions compliance            |
| Consumer Protection                | Transparent pricing and warranty obligations         |

## 8. Competitive Forces (Porter's Five Forces)

### 8.1 Analysis Summary

| Force                        | Intensity | Assessment                                         |
|------------------------------|-----------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Threat of New Entrants       | Medium    | Low barriers for basic tools, high for full platform |
| Buyer Bargaining Power       | High      | Many alternatives including paper/free solutions    |
| Supplier Bargaining Power    | Low       | Cloud infrastructure is commoditized                 |
| Threat of Substitutes        | Medium    | Paper, Excel, WhatsApp are entrenched substitutes   |
| Competitive Rivalry          | Low       | No dominant Saudi-specific automotive DMS exists    |

### 8.2 Implications

| Force                        | Strategic Response                                   |
|------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| New Entrants                 | Maintain feature breadth (191+ screens, 13 domains)  |
| Buyer Power                  | Demonstrate clear ROI, lower switching cost anxiety  |
| Supplier Power               | Multi-cloud architecture for leverage                |
| Substitutes                  | ZATCA compliance as forcing function for adoption    |
| Rivalry                      | First-mover advantage in Saudi-specific DMS          |
