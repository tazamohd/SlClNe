## 1. Introduction

This document provides a standardized template for creating customer success stories and includes two fictional case study examples for sales enablement purposes. All case studies should follow the structure defined in Section 2 and adhere to the formatting and approval guidelines in Section 3.

## 2. Case Study Template Structure

Every SALIS AUTO case study must include the following sections:

### 2.1 Required Sections

| Headline            | Compelling result-oriented title                                | 1 sentence      |

| Solution            | How SALIS AUTO was implemented and which modules were deployed  | 2-3 paragraphs  |

## 4. Case Study Example 1: Al-Rashid Auto Services (Riyadh)

### 4.1 Headline

**Al-Rashid Auto Services Increases Throughput by 30% and Achieves Full ZATCA Compliance Across 5 Branches with SALIS AUTO**

### 4.2 Company Profile

| Field                    | Value                                                |
|--------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Company Name             | Al-Rashid Auto Services                              |
| Location                 | Riyadh, Central Region                               |
| Industry Segment         | Multi-brand independent workshop group               |
| Branches                 | 5 (3 Riyadh, 1 Kharj, 1 Qassim)                     |
| Employees                | 85                                                   |
| Bays                     | 28 total                                             |
| Monthly Job Volume       | 1,800 jobs/month                                     |
| SALIS AUTO Since         | January 2026                                         |
| Tier                     | Professional                                         |

### 4.3 Challenge

Al-Rashid Auto Services had grown from a single workshop in Riyadh's Al-Sulay industrial district to five branches across three cities over eight years. However, the company's management systems had not kept pace with its expansion.

Each branch operated independently using a combination of paper job cards, Excel spreadsheets for financial tracking, and WhatsApp groups for communication between advisors and managers. The owner, Mohammed Al-Rashid, had no consolidated view of operations across his five branches. Monthly financial reporting required a full-time accountant to manually consolidate data from each location, a process that took 5-7 working days.

The ZATCA Phase 2 e-invoicing mandate presented an immediate compliance challenge. Al-Rashid had been manually issuing invoices and had no system capable of generating compliant e-invoices with QR codes, hash chains, or Fatoora portal integration. The company faced potential penalties of SAR 5,000 or more per non-compliant invoice.

Customer experience was suffering as well. Estimate approvals averaged 2-3 days because advisors had to phone customers, wait for callbacks, and then relay approvals manually to the workshop floor. Customers frequently complained about lack of visibility into their vehicle's repair status.

### 4.4 Solution

SALIS AUTO was implemented across all five Al-Rashid branches in a phased rollout over six weeks. The Professional tier was selected to provide full multi-branch management, ZATCA Phase 2 compliance, and AI platform access.

**Phase 1 (Weeks 1-2)**: Core setup including branch configuration, user accounts for 85 employees across 8 roles, and data migration of 12,000 customer records and 8,500 vehicle records from Excel files.

**Phase 2 (Weeks 3-4)**: Workshop workflow activation with digital job cards, multi-point inspections, and the estimate-to-approval workflow. Service advisors and technicians received hands-on training at each branch.

**Phase 3 (Weeks 5-6)**: Finance module activation with ZATCA Phase 2 configuration, Fatoora portal integration, and the Customer App rollout enabling digital estimate approvals and e-signatures.

Key modules deployed: Workshop, Registry, Finance, Accounting, Parts & Inventory, Reports & Analytics, AI Platform, and the Customer App portal.

### 4.5 Results

- **30% increase in daily job throughput** across all branches (from 60 to 78 jobs/day aggregate)
- **100% ZATCA Phase 2 compliance** from day one of finance module activation
- **Estimate approval time reduced from 2-3 days to 3 hours** via Customer App notifications
- **Invoice processing time reduced from 12 minutes to 90 seconds** per invoice
- **Real-time consolidated dashboard** replacing 5-7 days of manual monthly reporting
- **SAR 180,000 annual savings** in administrative labor across five branches
- **Customer NPS improved from 32 to 61** within four months of Customer App deployment
- **Zero ZATCA penalties** since deployment (previously averaging 2-3 per quarter)

### 4.6 Key Metrics

| Metric                      | Before SALIS AUTO | After SALIS AUTO | Change          |
|-----------------------------|--------------------|--------------------|-----------------|
| Daily Jobs (all branches)   | 60                 | 78                 | +30%            |
| Estimate Approval Time      | 2-3 days           | 3 hours            | -94%            |
| Invoice Processing          | 12 minutes         | 90 seconds         | -88%            |
| Monthly Reporting Time      | 5-7 days           | Real-time          | Eliminated      |
| ZATCA Compliance            | Non-compliant      | 100%               | Full compliance |
| Customer NPS                | 32                 | 61                 | +29 points      |

### 4.7 Testimonial

> "Before SALIS AUTO, I was running five workshops but I could not tell you how any of them was performing until my accountant finished the monthly report a week into the next month. Now I open my phone and see every branch, every bay, every riyal in real time. The ZATCA compliance alone justified the investment -- we were heading for serious penalties. But the real transformation has been in how our customers experience our service. They approve estimates on their phones, they track their car's progress, and they sign digitally when they pick up. We look like a modern business because we are one now."
>
> -- Mohammed Al-Rashid, Owner, Al-Rashid Auto Services

## 5. Case Study Example 2: Gulf Fleet Maintenance (Jeddah)

### 5.1 Headline

**Gulf Fleet Maintenance Improves Fleet Utilization by 35% and Reduces Procurement Cycle by 45% Using SALIS AUTO Enterprise**

### 5.2 Company Profile

| Field                    | Value                                                |
|--------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Company Name             | Gulf Fleet Maintenance                               |
| Location                 | Jeddah, Western Region                               |
| Industry Segment         | Fleet maintenance provider                           |
| Branches                 | 2 (Jeddah Industrial City, Jeddah North)             |
| Employees                | 45                                                   |
| Bays                     | 16 total                                             |
| Monthly Job Volume       | 600 jobs/month                                       |
| Fleet Contracts          | 12 active fleet contracts (380 vehicles)             |
| SALIS AUTO Since         | March 2026                                           |
| Tier                     | Enterprise                                           |

### 5.3 Challenge

Gulf Fleet Maintenance (GFM) operates as a dedicated fleet maintenance provider serving ride-hailing companies, delivery fleets, and car rental operators in the Jeddah metropolitan area. The company manages maintenance for 380 vehicles across 12 fleet contracts with strict SLA requirements including maximum vehicle downtime of 48 hours for standard maintenance and 72 hours for major repairs.

GFM's primary operational challenge was fleet utilization visibility. Fleet managers had no centralized system to track which vehicles were in service, scheduled for maintenance, or awaiting parts. Contract SLA compliance was monitored manually using spreadsheets, leading to frequent disputes with fleet clients about downtime calculations.

Procurement was a critical bottleneck. GFM's parts manager maintained relationships with 15 suppliers but had no systematic way to compare prices, track order status, or forecast parts demand. Emergency parts orders (at premium pricing) accounted for 25% of total parts spend because stock levels were monitored manually and reorder points were frequently missed.

Financial operations were strained by the volume and complexity of fleet billing. Each fleet contract had different rate structures, and generating accurate monthly invoices required 3-4 days of manual calculation per contract. ZATCA Phase 2 compliance added another layer of complexity to an already burdened process.

### 5.4 Solution

SALIS AUTO Enterprise was deployed at both GFM locations over four weeks with an emphasis on fleet management, parts and inventory, and finance workflows.

**Configuration highlights**:
- Fleet Manager Portal configured for 12 fleet client accounts with contract-specific SLA parameters
- Parts & Inventory module loaded with 4,200 SKUs across 15 supplier catalogs with automated reorder points
- Smart Scheduling activated for predictive maintenance scheduling based on fleet mileage data
- Finance module configured with contract-specific billing templates and ZATCA Phase 2 integration

**Integration points**: GFM's fleet clients received Fleet Manager Portal access, providing real-time visibility into their vehicles' maintenance status, cost tracking, and SLA compliance dashboards.

### 5.5 Results

- **35% improvement in fleet utilization** measured by vehicle-days-in-service vs. total vehicle-days
- **45% reduction in procurement cycle** from 5 days average to 2.75 days
- **Emergency parts orders reduced from 25% to 8%** of total parts spend through automated reorder points
- **Monthly fleet billing reduced from 3-4 days to 4 hours** per contract using billing templates
- **100% ZATCA Phase 2 compliance** with automated e-invoice generation for all fleet invoices
- **SLA compliance visibility improved** with real-time dashboards replacing manual spreadsheet tracking
- **SAR 320,000 annual parts savings** from price comparison and reduced emergency procurement
- **Two new fleet contracts won** attributed to the professional Fleet Manager Portal experience

### 5.6 Key Metrics

| Metric                      | Before SALIS AUTO | After SALIS AUTO | Change          |
|-----------------------------|--------------------|--------------------|-----------------|
| Fleet Utilization           | 62%                | 84%                | +35%            |
| Procurement Cycle           | 5 days             | 2.75 days          | -45%            |
| Emergency Parts Orders      | 25% of spend       | 8% of spend        | -68%            |
| Monthly Billing Time        | 3-4 days/contract  | 4 hours/contract   | -94%            |
| SLA Disputes                | 3-4 per quarter    | 0                  | Eliminated      |
| ZATCA Compliance            | Non-compliant      | 100%               | Full compliance |

### 5.7 Testimonial

> "Our fleet clients expect professional, transparent service. Before SALIS AUTO, we were managing SAR 15 million in annual fleet contracts with spreadsheets and WhatsApp. That is not sustainable and it is not the image we want to project. The Fleet Manager Portal changed our client relationships overnight -- they can see their vehicles' status in real time, and SLA disputes have dropped to zero. The parts procurement improvement alone saved us SAR 320,000 in the first year. SALIS AUTO has become our competitive advantage."
>
> -- Khalid Al-Juhani, General Manager, Gulf Fleet Maintenance
