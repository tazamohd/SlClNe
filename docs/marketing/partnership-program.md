## 1. Introduction

The SALIS AUTO Partnership Program enables third-party organizations to participate in the growth of automotive workshop digitization across Saudi Arabia. This document defines the partner types, commission structures, certification requirements, and co-marketing programs available to organizations that join the SALIS AUTO ecosystem.

The program is designed to scale SALIS AUTO's reach beyond direct sales capacity while ensuring consistent customer experience and implementation quality through structured partner enablement and tiered incentives.

## 4. Commission Structure

### 4.1 Referral Commissions

Referral partners earn a one-time commission on the first 12 months of subscription revenue from referred customers who convert.

| Tier       | Commission Rate | Example (Professional Annual)     |
|------------|-----------------|-----------------------------------|
| Bronze     | 10%             | SAR 25,490 x 10% = SAR 2,549     |
| Silver     | 12%             | SAR 25,490 x 12% = SAR 3,059     |
| Gold       | 15%             | SAR 25,490 x 15% = SAR 3,824     |
| Platinum   | 18%             | SAR 25,490 x 18% = SAR 4,588     |

### 4.2 Reseller Margins

Reseller partners purchase subscriptions at a discounted rate and sell at list price (or their own pricing up to list).

| Tier       | Discount off List | Effective Margin (Professional)   |
|------------|-------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Bronze     | 15%               | SAR 375/month per customer        |
| Silver     | 20%               | SAR 500/month per customer        |
| Gold       | 25%               | SAR 625/month per customer        |
| Platinum   | 30%               | SAR 750/month per customer        |

### 4.3 Implementation Partner Revenue

Implementation partners earn revenue from two streams:

| Revenue Stream               | Structure                                         |
|------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------|
| Implementation Services      | Partner sets their own rates (SALIS AUTO provides guidelines) |
| Recurring Referral Commission| 5% of subscription revenue for customer lifetime   |

### 4.4 Commission Payment Terms

| Parameter                  | Value                                              |
|----------------------------|----------------------------------------------------|
| Payment Trigger            | Customer's first invoice paid                      |
| Payment Frequency          | Monthly, 30 days after trigger event               |
| Minimum Payout             | SAR 500                                            |
| Payment Method             | Bank transfer to registered partner account        |
| Currency                   | SAR                                                |
| Tax                        | Partner responsible for own tax obligations        |
| Clawback Period            | 90 days (if customer cancels within 90 days)       |

## 8. Deal Registration

### 8.1 Deal Registration Process

| Step | Action                                           | Timeline          |
|------|--------------------------------------------------|-------------------|
| 1    | Partner registers opportunity in Partner Portal  | Before engagement |
| 2    | SALIS AUTO validates and approves registration   | Within 48 hours   |
| 3    | Partner receives exclusive engagement window     | Immediate         |
| 4    | Partner pursues opportunity with SALIS support   | Per sales cycle   |
| 5    | Deal closes and commission is calculated         | At contract sign  |
| 6    | Commission paid after customer payment           | Net 30 days       |

### 8.2 Deal Registration Rules

| Rule                                | Detail                                              |
|-------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Exclusivity Window                  | 90 days from registration approval                  |
| Renewal                             | One 60-day extension upon request with justification|
| Duplicate Registration              | First-to-register prevails                          |
| Existing Pipeline                   | SALIS AUTO reserves existing pipeline opportunities |
| Minimum Information                 | Company name, contact, size, timeline required      |
| Inactive Registration               | Auto-expired after 90 days with no activity        |

### 8.3 Deal Protection

| Scenario                            | Resolution                                          |
|-------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Two partners register same lead     | First registration with valid contact wins          |
| Partner lead overlaps SALIS pipeline| SALIS pipeline takes priority, partner notified     |
| Customer contacts SALIS directly    | Registered partner retains credit if within window  |
| Partner inactive on registered deal | Registration expires, opportunity becomes open      |
