## 1. Introduction

This guide helps workshop owners, managers, and accountants interpret the financial reports generated by the SALIS AUTO platform. It covers P&L analysis, revenue breakdown, accounts receivable management, cash flow forecasting, branch profitability, and ZATCA VAT reconciliation. All monetary values in the system are stored as integer halalas and displayed as SAR (1 SAR = 100 halalas). For ZATCA-specific compliance checks, see [ZATCA Compliance Checklist](./zatca-compliance-checklist.md). For role-based access to financial reports, refer to the [RBAC Matrix](../reference/rbac-matrix.md).

## 2. Profit & Loss Analysis by Service Category

### 2.1 Service Category Structure

SALIS AUTO organizes revenue and costs into the following service categories:

| Category Code | Category Name         | Typical Revenue Share |
|---------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| MAINT         | Preventive Maintenance| 25-30%               |
| REPAIR        | Mechanical Repair     | 30-35%               |
| TIRE          | Tire Services         | 10-15%               |
| BODY          | Body & Paint          | 10-15%               |
| ELEC          | Electrical / Diagnostic| 8-12%               |
| AC            | AC Services           | 5-10%                |
| OTHER         | Miscellaneous         | 2-5%                 |

### 2.2 P&L Line Items per Category

| Line Item                  | Description                                |
|----------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| Gross Revenue              | Total invoiced amount (parts + labor)      |
| Parts Cost (COGS)          | Cost of parts consumed                     |
| Labor Cost                 | Technician wages allocated to category     |
| Gross Profit               | Revenue - Parts Cost - Labor Cost          |
| Gross Margin %             | Gross Profit / Revenue x 100               |
| Direct Overhead            | Bay rental, equipment depreciation         |
| Category Net Profit        | Gross Profit - Direct Overhead             |

### 2.3 Margin Targets by Category

| Category              | Target Gross Margin | Typical Parts % | Typical Labor % |
|-----------------------|--------------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Preventive Maintenance| 55-65%             | 30%             | 70%             |
| Mechanical Repair     | 40-50%             | 45%             | 55%             |
| Tire Services         | 25-35%             | 70%             | 30%             |
| Body & Paint          | 45-55%             | 40%             | 60%             |
| Electrical / Diagnostic| 50-60%            | 25%             | 75%             |
| AC Services           | 45-55%             | 50%             | 50%             |

### 2.4 Interpreting Category P&L

When reviewing category-level P&L:

- **Margin below target**: Investigate parts cost (supplier pricing, waste) and labor efficiency (time vs. estimate)
- **Revenue share shift**: If a category's share drops significantly, check if demand changed or if advisors are under-recommending services
- **Seasonal variation**: AC services will spike in summer, maintenance may dip during Ramadan. Compare year-over-year, not just month-over-month
- **Negative margin**: Immediate investigation required. Common causes include warranty rework not billed, incorrect parts pricing, or labor estimate underruns

## 4. Accounts Receivable Aging

### 4.1 Aging Buckets

SALIS AUTO uses standard aging buckets for outstanding receivables:

| Bucket          | Age Range       | Risk Level  | Action Required              |
|-----------------|----------------|-------------|------------------------------|
| Current         | 0-30 days      | Low         | Standard follow-up           |
| 30+ days        | 31-60 days     | Medium      | Reminder call/SMS            |
| 60+ days        | 61-90 days     | High        | Escalate to manager          |
| 90+ days        | 91-180 days    | Very High   | Collection procedures        |
| 180+ days       | Over 180 days  | Write-off risk | Legal review, provision   |

### 4.2 AR Targets

| Metric                        | Target            |
|-------------------------------|-------------------|
| Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)  | Below 30 days     |
| Current bucket %              | 75%+ of total AR  |
| 90+ days %                    | Below 10% of AR   |
| Bad debt write-off rate       | Below 2% of revenue|
| Collection effectiveness      | 95%+              |

### 4.3 Customer Credit Management

| Action                          | Role Required | Approval Limit (SAR) |
|---------------------------------|--------------|----------------------|
| Set customer credit limit       | Manager      | Up to 50,000         |
| Extend credit terms             | Manager      | Up to 90 days        |
| Override credit hold            | Owner        | Unlimited            |
| Write off bad debt              | Owner        | Unlimited            |
| Issue credit note               | Accountant   | Up to 25,000         |

Note: Setting a customer credit limit and issuing a credit note form a Separation of Duties (SOD) pair. The same user must not perform both actions.

### 4.4 AR Aging Report Interpretation

Run Finance > Reports > AR Aging weekly. Key things to look for:

- **Large balances in 60+ bucket**: Contact the customer immediately; escalate fleet/corporate accounts to management
- **Recurring late payers**: Consider reducing credit limits or switching to cash terms
- **Insurance receivables**: Track separately; typical settlement is 45-60 days
- **Disputed invoices**: Flag and investigate within 48 hours to prevent aging

### 5.3 Forecasting Tips

- **Seasonal adjustments**: Reduce projected revenue by 30-40% during Ramadan. Increase AC service revenue by 60-80% in summer months
- **Payroll timing**: Saudi payroll runs on the Gregorian calendar (typically 25th-28th). Account for this fixed outflow
- **VAT payment**: Quarterly VAT returns due by the last day of the month following the quarter end. Budget for the 15% remittance
- **Parts payment terms**: If you have 30-day terms with suppliers, your parts outflow lags consumption by a month
- **Minimum cash buffer**: Maintain at least 2 weeks of operating expenses as a cash buffer

### 6.4 Underperforming Branch Analysis

When a branch consistently falls below targets:

1. Compare its cost structure to similar-sized branches
2. Analyze revenue mix -- is the service mix sub-optimal for the location?
3. Review staffing levels relative to demand
4. Assess local competition and pricing pressure
5. Check customer retention and NPS for branch-specific issues
6. Evaluate the branch manager's operational discipline

See [Multi-Branch Management](./multi-branch-management.md) for operational strategies.

## 8. ZATCA VAT Reconciliation

### 8.1 Monthly VAT Reconciliation Process

| Step | Action                                              | Responsible |
|------|-----------------------------------------------------|-------------|
| 1    | Export all invoices for the period from SALIS AUTO   | Accountant  |
| 2    | Calculate total output VAT (VAT collected on sales)  | Accountant  |
| 3    | Calculate total input VAT (VAT paid on purchases)    | Accountant  |
| 4    | Net VAT = Output VAT - Input VAT                     | Accountant  |
| 5    | Cross-reference with ZATCA portal submission records | Accountant  |
| 6    | Identify and resolve any discrepancies                | Accountant  |
| 7    | Manager review and sign-off                           | Manager     |
| 8    | Archive reconciliation records                        | Accountant  |

### 8.2 Common Reconciliation Discrepancies

| Discrepancy                    | Likely Cause                        | Resolution                    |
|-------------------------------|-------------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Output VAT mismatch           | Credit notes not included           | Include all credit/debit notes|
| Input VAT mismatch            | Missing purchase invoices           | Collect from suppliers        |
| Timing differences            | Invoice issued in one period, reported in another | Align to invoice date |
| Rounding differences          | Different rounding methods          | Use halala-based integer math |
| Rejected invoices             | ZATCA rejected submission           | Resubmit corrected invoices  |
| Zero-rated misclassification  | Standard items coded as zero-rated  | Review tax code assignments   |

### 8.3 VAT Return Schedule

| Filing Period | Frequency   | Due Date                              |
|---------------|-------------|---------------------------------------|
| Monthly       | Monthly     | Last day of following month           |
| Quarterly     | Quarterly   | Last day of month following quarter   |

Filing frequency depends on annual revenue:

| Annual Revenue (SAR)    | Filing Frequency |
|------------------------|------------------|
| Over 40 million        | Monthly          |
| Under 40 million       | Quarterly        |

### 8.4 VAT Reconciliation Checklist

- [ ] All sales invoices for the period are included
- [ ] All credit notes are deducted from output VAT
- [ ] All debit notes are added to output VAT
- [ ] Input VAT includes only tax invoices with valid TIN
- [ ] No duplicate invoices in the calculation
- [ ] VAT amounts match the ZATCA submission records
- [ ] Net VAT payable matches the VAT return amount
- [ ] Supporting documents archived per 7-year retention rule

## 9. Monthly Close Checklist

### 9.1 Pre-Close Activities (Last 3 Business Days of Month)

- [ ] All completed jobs have been invoiced
- [ ] All parts receipts are recorded in inventory
- [ ] All customer payments are posted and reconciled
- [ ] All supplier invoices are entered
- [ ] Bank statement downloaded and available for reconciliation
- [ ] Petty cash counted and reconciled

### 9.2 Close Activities (First 3 Business Days of Following Month)

- [ ] Revenue recognition review -- all invoiced jobs match delivered services
- [ ] Parts COGS reconciliation -- inventory consumption matches job cards
- [ ] Payroll processed and posted
- [ ] Depreciation entries posted
- [ ] Prepaid expenses amortized
- [ ] Accruals reviewed and adjusted
- [ ] Bank reconciliation completed
- [ ] Inter-branch transactions eliminated (for consolidated view)
- [ ] VAT reconciliation completed (see Section 8)
- [ ] Trial balance reviewed for unusual items

### 9.3 Post-Close Activities (Within First Week)

- [ ] Monthly P&L generated and reviewed per Section 2
- [ ] Branch profitability comparison prepared per Section 6
- [ ] AR aging report reviewed and collection actions assigned per Section 4
- [ ] Cash flow forecast updated per Section 5
- [ ] KPI dashboard updated and distributed to management
- [ ] Variance analysis prepared for any metric exceeding 10% deviation from budget
- [ ] Monthly close package submitted to owner/superadmin for review

### 9.4 Close Calendar

| Day of Month  | Activity                                        |
|---------------|------------------------------------------------|
| Last day - 3  | Begin pre-close reconciliations                |
| Last day      | Cut-off: no backdated entries without approval |
| 1st           | Bank reconciliation, payroll posting           |
| 2nd           | Accruals, depreciation, adjustments            |
| 3rd           | Trial balance review, P&L generation           |
| 5th           | Monthly close package distributed              |
| 7th           | Management review meeting                      |
