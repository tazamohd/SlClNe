## 1. Department Overview

The Sales & Marketing department drives customer acquisition, brand awareness, and revenue growth for the SALIS AUTO platform across the Saudi Arabian automotive workshop market. The department targets independent workshops, multi-branch chains, dealership service centers, and fleet maintenance operations as platform tenants.

The department is responsible for the full revenue pipeline from lead generation through deal closure and handoff to Customer Service for onboarding. Marketing focuses on establishing SALIS AUTO as the leading workshop management SaaS in KSA, leveraging digital channels, industry events, and strategic partnerships.

**Primary Responsibilities:**
- Lead generation and qualification
- Product demonstrations and trials
- Proposal and contract negotiation
- Revenue target achievement and forecasting
- Brand awareness and market positioning
- Content marketing and thought leadership
- Trade show and event participation
- Partner and channel development

## 7. Sales Process

### 7.1 Pipeline Stages

```
[LEAD] → [QUALIFIED] → [DEMO] → [PROPOSAL] → [NEGOTIATION] → [CLOSED WON] → [HANDOFF]
  |          |             |          |              |               |             |
 SDR       BANT          AE         AE             AE            AE/Mgr      AE → CS
 source    check        45 min    custom         terms          contract    onboarding
                                  proposal       review         signed       begins
```

### 7.2 Stage Definitions and Exit Criteria

| Stage         | Definition                              | Exit Criteria                         | Avg. Days |
|---------------|-----------------------------------------|---------------------------------------|-----------|
| Lead          | Contact identified, minimal info        | Name, company, phone/email confirmed  | 1-3       |
| Qualified     | BANT criteria assessed                  | Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline OK  | 3-7       |
| Demo          | Product demonstrated                   | Demo completed, interest confirmed    | 5-10      |
| Proposal      | Custom proposal sent                   | Proposal reviewed by prospect         | 7-14      |
| Negotiation   | Terms under discussion                 | Pricing and terms agreed              | 7-21      |
| Closed Won    | Contract signed                        | Signed contract + first payment       | 1-3       |
| Handoff       | Transition to Customer Service         | Onboarding kickoff scheduled          | 1-2       |

### 7.3 BANT Qualification Framework

| Criterion   | Qualifying Questions                                      | Pass Threshold              |
|-------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------|
| **B**udget  | What is your monthly software budget?                     | > SAR 999/month             |
| **A**uthority| Who makes the final purchasing decision?                 | Decision maker identified   |
| **N**eed    | What problems are you trying to solve?                    | 2+ pain points identified   |
| **T**imeline| When do you plan to implement a solution?                 | Within 6 months             |
