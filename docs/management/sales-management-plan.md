## 1. Executive Summary

This plan defines the sales strategy, organizational structure, quotas,
pipeline management, and enablement programs for SALIS AUTO. The platform
targets Saudi Arabia's 17,300+ automotive workshops across four market
segments. With ZATCA Phase 2 e-invoicing compliance creating a regulatory
forcing function, our sales motion leverages urgency-driven messaging and
a consultative enterprise approach to drive adoption of the only
Arabic-first, ZATCA-native workshop management solution covering all 13
operational domains across 191+ screens.

Year 1 revenue target: SAR 1.2M ARR from 50 customers.
Year 3 revenue target: SAR 12M ARR from 200+ customers across three cities.

| Western  | WR   | Jeddah, Makkah, Madinah, Taif   | 5,800 workshops  | P1       |

| Southern | SR   | Abha, Jizan, Najran             | 1,600 workshops  | P2       |

### 3.2 Territory Assignment Rules

1. **Named accounts**: Enterprise accounts (20+ bays) are assigned by VP Sales
   directly to senior AEs regardless of geography.
2. **Geographic assignment**: All other leads are routed by city to the
   assigned regional team.
3. **Round-robin within region**: SDRs and AEs within a region alternate
   inbound lead assignment.
4. **Expansion regions (P2)**: Northern and Southern regions are covered
   remotely by the Central and Western teams respectively until headcount
   justifies dedicated assignment (target: Q2 2028).

## 4. Target Market Segmentation

### 4.1 Segment Profiles

| Segment              | Bay Range | Decision Maker     | Budget Range (SAR/mo) | Sales Motion     |
|----------------------|-----------|--------------------|------------------------|------------------|
| Independent Workshop | 1-5 bays  | Owner/Operator     | 999-1,499              | Self-serve + SDR |
| Multi-Branch Chain   | 6-20 bays | Operations Manager | 2,499-4,999            | Consultative     |
| Franchise Dealer     | 20+ bays  | IT Director/CFO    | 5,000-15,000           | Enterprise       |
| Fleet Operator       | N/A       | Fleet Manager      | 2,499-9,999            | Solution sell    |

### 4.2 Segment-Specific Value Propositions

1. **Independent workshops**: ZATCA compliance without hiring an accountant;
   Arabic-first interface with zero training time; estimate-to-invoice in
   under 2 minutes.
2. **Multi-branch chains**: Centralized dashboard across all branches; 14
   role-based access controls; consolidated inventory and procurement across
   locations.
3. **Franchise dealers**: OEM integration capability; enterprise SLA; custom
   reporting; dedicated account management; white-label options.
4. **Fleet operators**: Preventive maintenance scheduling; fleet-wide cost
   tracking; vendor management; API integrations for fleet telematics.

### 4.3 Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

| Criteria              | Minimum Threshold                               |
|-----------------------|--------------------------------------------------|

| Current systems       | Paper-based or spreadsheets (greenfield)         |
| ZATCA registration    | Active or in-progress                            |

## 5. Sales Process

### 5.1 Sales Stages and Definitions

| Stage          | Exit Criteria                                        | Owner | Avg Duration  |
|----------------|------------------------------------------------------|-------|---------------|
| 1. Lead        | Contact info validated, initial interest confirmed    | SDR   | 1-3 days      |
| 2. Qualified   | BANT confirmed (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline)   | SDR   | 3-5 days      |
| 3. Demo        | Live demo completed, stakeholders attended            | AE    | 3-7 days      |
| 4. Proposal    | Proposal sent with pricing and implementation plan    | AE    | 5-10 days     |
| 5. Negotiation | Terms agreed, legal/procurement review initiated      | AE/RM | 5-15 days     |
| 6. Closed Won  | Contract signed, payment method confirmed             | AE    | 1-3 days      |
| 7. Onboarding  | Tenant provisioned, data migrated, training complete  | CS    | 7-21 days     |

### 5.2 Average Sales Cycle by Segment

| Segment              | Average Cycle | Range        | Stakeholders Involved |
|----------------------|---------------|--------------|-----------------------|
| Independent Workshop | 3 weeks       | 2-4 weeks    | 1-2                   |
| Multi-Branch Chain   | 6 weeks       | 4-8 weeks    | 3-5                   |
| Franchise Dealer     | 12 weeks      | 8-16 weeks   | 5-10                  |
| Fleet Operator       | 8 weeks       | 6-12 weeks   | 3-6                   |

### 5.3 BANT Qualification Framework

- **Budget**: Has allocated or can allocate SAR 999-15,000/mo for operations
  software; understands TCO vs. manual process costs.
- **Authority**: Contact is owner, GM, or has documented purchasing authority;
  for enterprise, procurement sign-off process is identified.
- **Need**: Currently experiencing at least 2 of: ZATCA compliance gaps,
  manual estimate/invoice workflow, inventory tracking failures, multi-branch
  visibility issues.
- **Timeline**: Active project within 90 days; ZATCA compliance deadline or
  expansion/new-branch opening driving urgency.

## 6. Pipeline Management

### 6.1 Conversion Rates by Segment

| Stage Transition         | Independent | Chain  | Enterprise | Fleet  |
|--------------------------|-------------|--------|------------|--------|
| Lead → Qualified         | 40%         | 35%    | 25%        | 30%    |
| Qualified → Demo         | 70%         | 65%    | 60%        | 65%    |
| Demo → Proposal          | 60%         | 55%    | 50%        | 55%    |
| Proposal → Negotiation   | 50%         | 45%    | 40%        | 45%    |
| Negotiation → Closed Won | 70%         | 65%    | 55%        | 60%    |
| **Overall Lead→Close**   | **5.9%**    | **3.6%**| **1.7%**  | **2.9%**|

### 6.2 Pipeline Coverage Ratio

- **Target coverage**: 3x weighted pipeline to quota at all times.
- **Minimum coverage**: 2.5x triggers pipeline generation sprint.
- **Below 2x**: Escalation to VP Sales; SDR outbound volume doubled.

### 6.3 Weighted Pipeline Calculation

| Stage          | Probability Weight | Example: SAR 5,000 MRR Deal |
|----------------|--------------------|-----------------------------|
| Lead           | 5%                 | SAR 250                     |
| Qualified      | 15%                | SAR 750                     |
| Demo           | 30%                | SAR 1,500                   |
| Proposal       | 50%                | SAR 2,500                   |
| Negotiation    | 75%                | SAR 3,750                   |
| Verbal Commit  | 90%                | SAR 4,500                   |

### 6.4 Pipeline Review Cadence

| Meeting                | Frequency | Attendees               | Focus                           |
|------------------------|-----------|-------------------------|---------------------------------|
| SDR Stand-up           | Daily     | SDRs, Regional Manager  | Activity metrics, lead quality  |
| AE Pipeline Review     | Weekly    | AEs, Regional Manager   | Deal progression, blockers      |
| Regional Forecast      | Bi-weekly | RM, VP Sales            | Forecast accuracy, commit deals |

## 10. Key Metrics and KPIs

### 10.1 Revenue Metrics

| Metric                        | Definition                                   | Year 1 Target    |
|-------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|------------------|
| MRR (Monthly Recurring Rev)   | Total active subscription revenue per month  | SAR 100,000      |
| ARR (Annual Recurring Rev)    | MRR x 12                                     | SAR 1,200,000    |
| New MRR                       | MRR from new logos in the period             | SAR 8,000/mo avg |
| Expansion MRR                 | MRR from upgrades and add-ons               | SAR 2,000/mo avg |
| Churn MRR                     | MRR lost from cancellations                  | < SAR 1,500/mo   |
| NRR (Net Revenue Retention)   | (Starting MRR + Expansion - Churn) / Start  | > 110%           |

### 10.2 Sales Efficiency Metrics

| Metric                  | Definition                              | Target         |
|-------------------------|-----------------------------------------|----------------|
| CAC (Customer Acq Cost) | Total sales+marketing spend / new logos | < SAR 15,000   |
| LTV (Lifetime Value)    | ARPU x Gross Margin x Avg Lifespan     | > SAR 75,000   |
| LTV:CAC Ratio           | LTV / CAC                              | > 5:1          |
| Sales Cycle Length       | Days from lead to closed-won           | < 45 days avg  |
| Win Rate                | Closed-Won / (Closed-Won + Closed-Lost)| > 25%          |
| Churn Rate (monthly)    | Lost customers / Starting customers    | < 2%           |
| Payback Period           | CAC / (ARPU x Gross Margin)            | < 12 months    |
