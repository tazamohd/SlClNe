## 1. Financial Overview

This document presents the comprehensive financial plan for SALIS AUTO,
covering revenue forecasts, cost structures, profitability projections, and
key financial metrics over a five-year horizon. All figures are denominated
in Saudi Riyals (SAR) unless otherwise noted. VAT at 15% is excluded from
revenue figures (presented net) but included in cash flow considerations.
