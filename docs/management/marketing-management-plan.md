## 1. Executive Summary

This plan establishes the marketing strategy, brand positioning, channel mix,
budget allocation, and performance metrics for SALIS AUTO. As Saudi Arabia's
only Arabic-first, ZATCA-native automotive workshop management platform, SALIS
AUTO occupies a unique position at the intersection of regulatory compliance
and digital transformation. The marketing strategy leverages ZATCA Phase 2
compliance urgency as the primary demand driver while building long-term brand
authority across all 13 operational domains the platform covers.

Year 1 marketing budget: SAR 1,200,000.
Year 1 pipeline contribution target: 60% of total pipeline from marketing-sourced leads.

---

## 2. Brand Strategy

### 2.1 Brand Positioning Statement

> For Saudi automotive workshop owners and operators who need to modernize
> operations and comply with ZATCA e-invoicing mandates, SALIS AUTO is the
> complete workshop management platform that eliminates paperwork, automates
> compliance, and increases throughput -- unlike fragmented tools and generic
> ERPs, SALIS AUTO is purpose-built for Saudi workshops with Arabic-first
> design, 14 specialized roles, and end-to-end coverage across 191+ screens.

### 2.2 Brand Pillars

| Pillar          | Messaging Theme                                           |
|-----------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| ZATCA-Native    | Built-in Phase 2 compliance; no plugins, no workarounds   |
| Arabic-First    | Full RTL interface; designed in Arabic, translated to EN   |
| AI-Powered      | Smart diagnostics, predictive inventory, automated routing |
| All-in-One      | 13 domains, 28 RBAC modules, 191+ screens in one platform |

### 2.3 Brand Voice Guidelines

| Attribute    | Do                                           | Do Not                              |
|-------------|----------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Tone        | Professional, confident, empowering          | Aggressive, overly casual            |
| Language    | Arabic-first, simple business terminology    | Technical jargon, anglicisms         |
| Imagery     | Real Saudi workshops, diverse technicians    | Stock photos, Western workshop settings|
| Numerals    | SAR currency, Arabic-Indic numerals option   | USD references, Western-only formats |
