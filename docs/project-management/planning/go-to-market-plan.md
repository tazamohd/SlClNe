## 1. Purpose

This document defines the go-to-market (GTM) strategy for SALIS AUTO, a multi-tenant automotive workshop management SaaS platform designed for the Saudi Arabian market. It covers the phased market entry across Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dammam, channel strategy, marketing campaigns, partnership programs, and KPI targets with budget allocations in SAR.

### 2.1 Target Market

| SMB workshops     | 4-15 bays, 5-30 employees                      | 4,500+            |
| Enterprise chains | 15+ bays, multi-branch, corporate governance   | 800+              |
